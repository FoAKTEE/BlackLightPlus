BlackLight+

General-relativistic ray tracing, polarized radiative transfer and Monte Carlo Comptonization in Julia, on CPU threads or GPUs through one backend option. The package is BlackLightPlus.

A run is a plan, prepared once from a configuration, a spacetime and a matter source; every product of a plan runs on either backend with identical results, bitwise per pixel or per photon whatever the device count and chunking.

Capabilities

  • Spacetimes — Kerr in Cartesian Kerr–Schild coordinates; any analytic metric through a closure (UserMetric), differentiated by forward-mode AD; superposed Kerr–Schild binaries at rest (SuperposedKerrSchild) and in motion (BoostedSuperposedKerrSchild on a CircularOrbit), each hole's term in the rest frame of its velocity; numerical-relativity slices (ADMGrid) from AthenaK Z4c dumps with a $C^1$ interpolant.
  • Geodesics — Hamiltonian null geodesics in covariant momentum, $p_t$ conserved exactly on stationary metrics; a fixed-control RK4 march with the step $(r - r_h)/C$ for imaging and an adaptive Vern7 solve with event termination; an exact-Kerr elliptic path with lensing bands and photon-ring layers.
  • Radiative transfer — Stokes $I$ or full $IQUV$ in a parallel-transported camera frame with exact constant-coefficient segment operators; thermal, power-law and kappa synchrotron emission and absorption, Faraday rotation and conversion; the $R_{\rm high}/R_{\rm low}$ electron temperature; magnetization and temperature cuts.
  • Matter — AthenaK GRMHD snapshots (.athdf, .bin) sampled on their mesh hierarchy, uniform grids, slow light over a snapshot series, and analytic sources: a constant-angular-momentum torus, a Page–Thorne disk, an orbiting hotspot, a uniform sphere.
  • Products — images, adaptive image-plane refinement, trajectories, shadows, exact parameter derivatives of the image, mass-scale ladders from one trace, Monte Carlo spectra with Compton scattering.
  • Execution — one kernel for every device computation; Julia threads or a pool of CUDA devices chosen by backend; Float32 geodesic lanes and mixed lanes on Kerr.
