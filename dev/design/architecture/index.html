Architecture

The package BlackLightPlus (Julia ≥ 1.10) applies the principles of Design principles (P1–P18); test/structure.jl holds their mechanical gates. The reference implementation the oracle suite compares against (§6) is the previous package, kept with its oracle on the blp branch of the repository.

1. Three tiers, one direction

pipeline/   Plans · Analysis · Products            composition: configuration, plans,
                                                   image-plane analysis, products
    │  uses both tiers below
infra/      Data · Execution  (+ ext/BlackLightPlusCUDAExt)   machinery: file formats, fluid containers,
                                                   executors, device pool, residency, scheduling
    │  implements the protocols of, and uses, the tier below
physics/    Numerics · Spacetime · Matter ·        equations: pure, isbits after Adapt,
            Transfer · MonteCarlo                  allocation-free on the hot path, no threads

Rules, each a gate of test/structure.jl.

  • BlackLightPlus.jl includes the modules in the order Numerics → Spacetime → Matter → Transfer → MonteCarlo → Data → Execution → Plans → Analysis → Products; a module imports (using ..X, import ..X) only modules before it.
  • No physics module imports KernelAbstractions, HDF5, CUDA or Base.Threads. Threads.@threads appears only in the Data loaders and table builders (athenak_hdf5.jl, athenak_bin.jl, uniform_grid.jl, tables.jl); the executors run worker tasks that pull chunks from a queue.
  • There is exactly one @kernel, _foreach! in Execution/kernel.jl. Every device computation is a per-item program run through it.
  • src/ neither loads nor calls CUDA; ext/BlackLightPlusCUDAExt.jl adds the CUDADevice methods of the Execution device protocol. Nothing imports ext/.
  • A module's eponymous file holds all its includes and exports. A name with a leading underscore is never referenced outside its module, from src/ or from the tests.
  • No file of src/ exceeds 600 lines.

The physics tier defines the protocols (Spacetime, PlasmaProvider, TransferMode, the march-control functions, Sink); the infra tier supplies the grid-backed providers and the machinery that runs per-item programs; the pipeline tier turns a configuration into a plan, builds programs from it and hands them to an executor.

3. Physics tier

Everything here is pure, isbits (or isbits after Adapt), StaticArrays-typed and number-type generic, and allocates nothing on the hot path. Adapt only declares structure. The adaptive tracer trace_geodesic is the one host-only solver.

  • A march stops when the distance above the horizon proxy falls below HORIZON_EPS_SAMPLED (ADM: the lapse margin below zero) or is non-finite, when the normal-frame photon energy exceeds CAPTURE_REDSHIFT = 100, or when the ray is outbound beyond escape or R_em.   • ADMGrid is one struct for host and device: Adapt.adapt replaces data, and every method reads it through the same Catmull-Rom body. Its scalars share the data type, so it has no Float32 lane.

  • One tracker serves the sphere and the GRMHD fluid, with and without scattering. The control decides the step and the stop (FlatMarch: straight lines, h = step_frac R/|k|; CurvedMarch: RK4, h = (r − r_h)/(C |k|)) through the march-control functions of Transfer; the provider supplies the plasma through Matter.sample; absorb and scatter are compile-time switches.
  • rate_frame(provider) selects the absorption rule; the two rules agree in exact arithmetic on a null k and differ in rounding. Scattering is the biased exponential-transform decision scatter_event; may_scatter (state.valid) and scatter_site (the provider's sample) decide where and off what a photon scatters. The sphere scatters on every step that accrues depth, the leaving step included, and always kicks with its uniform plasma. A child's stream is child_rng(rng, event, layout).
  • bias returns 1 unless b0 (n + 1)² exceeds 1, a NaN product included. Constants carry no leading underscore. fluid_tetrad (imaging, e₍₃₎ along k) and emission_tetrad (Monte Carlo, e₍₁₎ along b) are different physics and stay apart.

4. Infrastructure tier

No physics is written here. This tier owns arrays, files, threads, devices, queues and caches, and implements the physics protocols over its containers.

4.2 Execution

abstract type Executor end

  • Programs. A program is an isbits-after-Adapt functor called as prog(item, i, outs...); it writes the outputs of item at outs[k][i] and returns nothing, or writes through atomics when it has no outputs. i is the item under threads and the position in the chunk on a device; after foreach_item!, outs[k][item] holds the outputs of item, bitwise the same whatever the executor, chunk size, device count, lane scheduling or order on one hardware (threads and host pools, or any number of CUDA devices); CPU and GPU outputs differ in their last bits (§6).
  • Chunks. An executor's chunk is an item count or :auto, the default, resolved per run by chunk_for from the item count n, the bytes of output per item and the program's chunk_hint (a trait beside workgroupsize, nothing by default): a pool on one device takes 2^20 items per launch; on d devices of l lanes clamp(cld(n, 2 l d), 2^16, 2^20); a pool never puts more than 16 MiB of outputs in one launch (but at least 2^16 items); threads take clamp(cld(n, 16 t), 16, 4096) on t threads; then at most the hint. The AMR worklist holds max(1, c ÷ block²) blocks per chunk and the Monte Carlo cascade sizes its own chunks (cascade_chunk). The resolved chunk of the last run is what describe(exec).chunk and a result's run record report. No result depends on the chunk.
  • Threads. ThreadedExecutor cuts the items into chunks in queue order and one worker task per Julia thread pulls the next chunk from one queue (run_workers!); each item calls the host program with the host arrays. A program's exception is rethrown as a CapturedException once the queue drains.
  • Pool. A DevicePool runs lanes persistent worker tasks per device pulling chunks from one shared queue. A chunk passes its items to the kernel (device_items: a range as it is, with no upload; any other list staged as Int32 when every item fits, Int otherwise), launches the program's device twin once (after prepare_launch!(dev, prog), which raises the CUDA stack to stack_bytes(prog), at workgroupsize(prog)), synchronizes and copies the outputs back through host staging into outs[k][item] (one contiguous copy for a range). Executors with the same devices and lanes share one pool of the process registry, whatever their chunk, closed at exit; a DevicePool built directly is the caller's to close.
  • Residency. DeviceAdaptor(pool, dev, readonly) is the Adapt adaptor of a device twin: each Array leaf becomes its device copy, cached per pool and device by the identity of the host array and held weakly (a cached host array must not be mutated afterwards). A program is adapted once per device and call with readonly = true, its arrays behind readonly(dev, a) (constify on a HostDevice, the read-only data cache on a CUDADevice). A type whose twin is not its arrays copied defines Adapt.adapt_structure(::DeviceAdaptor, x): the accumulators register zeroed twins; the worklist swaps the blocks of a BlockGrid per chunk.
  • Ray sources are program fields: CameraRays calls camera_ray inside the program, so device rays equal host rays. Scheduling (cost_order(curve, fov, npix; tile), rescue_pixels(curve, fov, npix; rescue_px), mixed_partition(items, rescue), march_sort_order, curve_inclination) is pure and evaluated by prepare.
  • Worklist. stream_blocks!(exec, prog, work, block, visit; outtype) runs chunks of at most max(1, chunk ÷ block²) blocks; visit(blk, vals) returns the block's children, queued at once, with no level barrier. The visited set and every block's values do not depend on the executor or the completion order.
  • Accumulators. ExactBins(bands; nu_min = 1e8, nu_max = 1e16, n_ebins = 200) over PolarBands(n) or InclinationBands(incl_deg, half_width_deg) holds per row and frequency bin the count and the limbs of Σw and ΣwE; record!(bins, E, w, dir) takes the escape direction and adds through atomics on either executor; Spectrum(bins; plan, run) rounds each cell once and answers band, nu_centers, nuLnu, nuLnu_total, nuLnu_solid, total_power, total_weight, mean_energy. InclinationBands keeps its band centres and half-width. ExactScalars(n) has add!, scalar, addends (one key or a tuple of keys). gather!(acc) adds the device twins after foreach_item! or foreach_cascade!; Spectrum, scalar and children throw while twins are pending. The accumulators are not Sinks: a Monte Carlo sink forwards record!(sink, sph) to record!(bins, sph.E, sph.w, escape_direction(ctl, sph)) and spawn! to the child buffer.
  • Cascade. foreach_cascade!(exec, primary, layer, n, depth; capacity, chunk) runs generation 0, primary over 1:n, then up to depth generations of layer over the children the generation before spawned, chunk by chunk: the worker that takes a chunk of chunk primaries (cascade_chunk(exec, n)) runs every generation of it before the next chunk. A program spawns through spawn!(buf, child, rng, event) into the ChildBuffer(T, capacity, layout) it holds (a slot of capacity 0 that supplies the element type and the stream layout) and layer reads its Generation(photons, rngs); the cascade swaps both in by adaptor. On a lane the chunk's children live in two device buffers swapped per generation and only their count is read back; a generation that can spawn records into zeroed lane scratch, drained into the device's totals through the one kernel once its children fit, and a generation whose children overflow the buffer (overflow) runs again in full after the buffer grows to the count. On threads each task keeps two growing buffers and reruns only the item that overflowed. Child streams are keyed by genealogy, so a rerun spawns identical children, and the totals, the set of children and the returned count are bitwise independent of the executor, the chunking, the device count, the lane and the arrival order.
  • Devices. HostDevice(id) (the KernelAbstractions CPU backend as a logical device) and CUDADevice(ordinal), an integer meaning the latter. The protocol is backend(dev), activate!, functional, stage_alloc/stage_free, free!, prepare_launch!, readonly, upload and select_devices; the fallbacks serve HostDevice and throw BackendUnavailable for other devices. The extension supplies the CUDA methods, page-locked staging freed explicitly and the NVML idle probe included.
  • Session state. The backend session record and the pool registry are the package's only session state; both sit behind locks.

The spacetime, the matter, the transfer mode and the lanes are values passed to prepare, never configuration fields. prepare derives everything once: the camera, its frame and pixel centres; the transfer parameters (units, electron model, the cuts sigma ≤ sigma_cut and theta_e ≥ theta_e_min through legacy_bound, a population per nonzero fraction); escape = escape_factor × distance; R_em = domain_radius(matter), Inf with matter = nothing; the march budget on both lanes; the horizon proxy (NaN when the spacetime has none); theta_o; on Kerr, the critical curve at curve_inclination and the cost-ordered queue (identity order elsewhere), or the queue given, a permutation of the pixel indices validated by queue_items (for a movie, march_sort_order(vec(shadow(plan).steps)) of the previous frame); the rescue set of MixedLanes. It throws ArgumentError for a spacetime that is not Float64, a geodesic lane the spacetime does not have, transfer or accumulator lanes other than Float64, MixedLanes off Kerr, and a queue that is not a permutation. A plan is immutable and backend-free; its one memo, hotcross_table(plan), builds the Monte Carlo hot-cross table at the plan's first scattering run and keeps it, so later runs reuse the table and its device copies. prepare(sphere) is the plan of the Monte Carlo sphere: Minkowski(), the sphere as matter in centimetres, RunConfig() and no parameter set; output_type and imaging_program reject such a plan.

function (p::ImagingProgram)(item, i, out)
    y0, ok = camera_state(p.rays, item)        # @noinline; the Float64 camera ray
    @inbounds out[i] = ok ? march(p.mode, lane_state(p.control, y0), p) : invalid(eltype(out))
    return nothing
end
workgroupsize(::ImagingProgram{IQUV}) = 64
  • imaging_program(plan; mode, params, gen) sets st = plan.spacetime, control = SpacetimeMarch(plan.geodesic_spacetime, plan.geodesic_budget) (the Float64 pair for the rescue lane) and rays = CameraRays(plan.spacetime, plan.frame, gen): camera rays are made on the Float64 spacetime and narrowed to the control's lane. A ray without a positive-energy null root gets invalid(T), NaN in every component.
  • The ray is computed in the non-inlined camera_state, and no program call operator carries @inline, so a program body is compiled as a function of its own and the threaded executor and the host-device pool run the same floating-point contractions.
  • TraceProgram(st, budget, rays, resume) marches one window of at most TRACE_WINDOW = 20 steps into per-item TraceStep{T}(y, h) outputs and a TraceState{T}(y, r_prev, done, live, ok) from which the next window resumes exactly; TRACE_WINDOW + 2 output arrays keep the call below the argument count at which Julia boxes arguments. ShadowProgram(st, budget, escape, rays) records fate, step count, largest null error and final radius and state. AnalyticProgram(gen, theta_o, a, ro, disk, bands) (in analytic.jl) lands every band of one screen point into the 5K plain Float64 outputs of analytic_outputs(K, n), quantity-major, which render_analytic reshapes into its maps without a copy; it needs a 32 KiB device stack.
  • The Monte Carlo programs MCPrimary(source, transport, sink, seed) and MCLayer(generation, transport, sink) (a Generation, or photons, rngs) are called as prog(item, i) without outputs and write through MCSink(ctl, orders, children, ledger), a Sink over the exact accumulators: SphereSource(sphere, tables) or ZoneSource(zones, weights, dnmax, offset, lint) generates the primaries, MCTransport(ctl, st, provider, opac, absorb, scatter) holds the fixed inputs of every march, and the ledger is striped LEDGER_STRIPES ways so the atomics spread over distinct addresses.
  • StoredPathProgram (a march along a stored trajectory) and AdaptiveTraceProgram (trace_geodesic with every step saved) hold host vectors and run on threads only. Every other program is isbits after Adapt and allocates nothing.

5.4 Choosing CPU or GPU

Where a product runs is one keyword, backend, with a session default and an environment override. A plan never records a backend: the same plan renders on the CPU and on the GPUs, and device uploads are cached by the pool.

PrecedenceFormExample
1keyword on the product callrender(plan; backend = :gpu)
2session defaultbackend!(:gpu); backend!([0, 1]); backend!(PoolExecutor([0, 1]; chunk = 2^15)); backend!(nothing) clears it
3BLP_BACKEND, read once at first useauto, cpu, gpu, gpu:0,1, host_pool, host_pool:2
4:autothe pool over select_devices() when CUDA is loaded, functional and a device is idle, else threads

resolve accepts nothing, :auto, :cpu (ThreadedExecutor()), :gpu, :host_pool (a pool over HostDevice(1)), an Executor, a DevicePool, a device or an ordinal, a list of devices or ordinals, and a string in the BLP_BACKEND grammar.

  • :auto never downgrades silently: its first resolution in a session logs the choice and the reason (CUDA not loaded, no functional device, no idle device, or the devices used).
  • An explicit choice is strict. :gpu without the CUDA extension, a functional device or a visible device throws BackendUnavailable naming the remedy (using CUDA, CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES, or backend!(:cpu)); its device selection is kept for the session. backend! resolves at once, so an unusable choice throws when it is set. A malformed BLP_BACKEND is an ArgumentError and is read again at the next use.
  • backend() returns the resolved session default; an executor prints its kind, devices, lanes per device, chunk and Julia threads. Execution.reset_backend!(), a test hook, forgets the session default, the environment reading, the cached :auto and :gpu choices and the log; registered pools are kept.
  • Device knobs (lanes, chunk) exist only on PoolExecutor and DevicePool; ThreadedExecutor has chunk. Precision lanes are a plan property, independent of the backend. Julia activates the extension only after the user's own using CUDA.

6. Continuity with the reference implementation

BlackLightPlus, the package in src/, is the reference implementation. The oracle suite test/oracle/ loads both packages, evaluates the reference device files into a scratch module to run them on host devices, and compares BlackLightPlus with the reference function by function and product by product on the decks of test/decks.jl.

  • Bitwise. Every Numerics function; every Spacetime method on Kerr, binaries, an ADM grid, a user metric and flat space (camera rays and exact-Kerr landings with their partials included); the Data samplers and readers, decode and Analysis; the analytic sources' scalars and validity; the transfer fits, tetrads and segment operators; the Monte Carlo emissivities, hot cross section, generation, Compton kick and sphere tracker with its children and streams. Stokes I images on every path: uniform grid, native blocks, coordinate lists, the superposed binary, slow light on both providers, mixed lanes, the mass-scale ladder, the :mass_scale sensitivity image, the exact-Kerr maps, photon-ring layers, AMR mosaics with their level maps and ray counts, and shadow fates and step counts.
  • Round-off natively, bitwise without FMA. IQUV images, traces, the shadows' null errors and final states, the :rat_high sensitivity image, the transfer functions on Dual states and the Monte Carlo torus zones and marches agree to about 1e-12 to 1e-9 relative. On FMA hardware LLVM fuses muladd pairs of the StaticArrays products into FMA instructions according to the inlining context, which differs between the two packages. The transfer, products and montecarlo scripts rerun themselves under julia -C generic, a CPU target without FMA (BLP_ORACLE_NOFMA=1), where each of these comparisons must be bitwise.
  • Round-off. The analytic sources' four-vectors and hot-spot scalars, for the same cause: recompiled with the metric pair passed in, the reference body deviates alike.
  • The two-phase path. BlackLightPlus's CPU two-phase IQUV render transports the camera frame along the stored states, the fused march with the geodesic; the two differ at about 2e-11 of the image maximum even without FMA. BlackLightPlus's renders against the reference's two-phase CPU render, and the sensitivity derivatives, must agree within 1e-7 of the image maximum; their deviations are reported.
  • Exact sums. Monte Carlo spectra are exact integer-limb sums: identical under 1, 3 and 8 threads, several chunkings and pools of 1, 2, 3 and 5 host devices.

Run the suite from the repository root:

julia --project=test/oracle -e 'using Pkg; Pkg.instantiate()'
julia -t 8 --project=test/oracle test/oracle/runtests.jl

Its environment develops both packages (BlackLightPlus/ and the repository root). The comparisons with the gpumonty hot-cross table read hotcross.dat from the path in BLP_REF_GPUMONTY, else from a Mahakala checkout beside the repository, and skip when it is absent. The device comparisons of the products, spectra and montecarlo scripts run on logical host devices; with BLP_TEST_DEVICES=cuda they run on the functional CUDA devices, the reference drivers and BlackLightPlus on the same device list, so an old-against-new comparison on the GPU itself is the same script. On the A100 the reference device drivers and BlackLightPlus are bitwise equal on every path, the Monte Carlo spectra included. A pool on the GPU against threads on the CPU is held at tolerance, not bitwise (Decks.CROSS_HARDWARE): the two targets contract floating-point operations differently: the images differ by 1e-13 to 3e-10 of their maximum on the oracle and production decks, and the exact Monte Carlo sums by 1e-16 (thin sphere) to 2e-12 (third scattering order) with equal photon counts. Timings of both targets, and of the reference package on the same decks (bitwise equal to BlackLightPlus on the same device), are in bench/ and Verification record §4.

9. Exports and internal interfaces

BlackLightPlus.jl exports the 54 names of §7, in its groups: configuration, spacetimes, matter, transfer modes, execution, products, results, analysis and I/O. They are the public API, under semantic versioning; the gate allows at most 54 names and at most 10 in the render/trace/shadow/mc_spectrum/sensitivity/variants/photon_ring_layers family. Each submodule exports its internal interface: the method tables of §3 and §4, the programs of §5.2, and Plan, transfer_params and geodesic_type of Plans. Every name a submodule exports is defined and has a docstring. Tests import from the submodules (using BlackLightPlus.Transfer: segment); no test or oracle script reaches a leading-underscore name.
