Verification record

The measured state of the package BlackLightPlus (version 0.3.0) on the host anta (2× Xeon Platinum 8360Y, 144 hardware threads; 2× A100 80 GB; Julia 1.12.6), as of 2026-10-18 on branch blp, whose evidence directories (results/blp/) this record cites. The standards these measurements are held to are in Architecture (§6 continuity, §10 tests) and test/structure.jl; the provenance of the paper's figures is in the manuscript's figures/CAPTIONS.md. This file records the results and the commands that reproduce them.

Three of the stored reference data sets no longer reproduce with the reference package at its current commit, while BlackLightPlus reproduces that package's rerun bitwise: the analytic disk and the spherical orbits (rounding of an earlier environment) and the Z4c ring and deck trajectories (the reference geodesic right-hand side changed at e253c10 after the published run). The summary figure's trajectory panel differs for that reason. Three products the figures needed are not entry points of BlackLightPlus and are assembled in the scripts from the submodule interfaces: a per-level barrier with a custom per-ray value in render_amr (the Z4c class refinement), ray classes per hole, and a coordinate-list ladder in variants.

What moved each leg, from the profiled call of each record (Performance §3 lists the capabilities behind them):

  • Exact-Kerr disk: the maps are written by the program in their final layout, so the host work after the last launch falls from 304 ms to 5 ms and the profiled call from 389 ms to 111 ms; the kernels take 56 ms in both packages, in 8 launches against 16. Its wall times vary by a factor two from call to call on both packages, from the collection of the previous call's 120 MB of maps (the reference's profiled call spends 193 ms before its first launch); its row is the best of seven calls of each package.
  • Scattering sphere: every scattering generation of a chunk runs on the lane that took it, with the children in two device buffers; the device idles 161 ms of a 805 ms call against 916 of 1,944 ms, in 72 launches against 182, and 0.5 ms of device-to-host copies against 134 ms.
  • Thin sphere, binary and native-block legs: the auto chunk is one launch per run of up to 2^20 items (10 launches against 153 on the thin sphere; one against four on the binary images), which is the reference's Monte Carlo chunk and the best explicit chunk of the earlier sweeps.
  • Mixed precision (0.92) and the best native-block IQUV (0.90) and binary 00455 I (0.89) rows are the remaining kernel-level differences: the Float32 lane runs at 226 registers against the reference's 132 (blocks of 256 against 384), and both IQUV kernels sit at the 255-register cap with BlackLightPlus's 16 percent slower at the same launch shape. A separate camera-ray launch, the reference's arrangement, leaves those register counts unchanged and is not faster, so the difference lies in the march itself and is tracked by the kernel audit (bench/kernel_audit.jl).
  • CPU imaging: BlackLightPlus marches each ray once, where the reference traces, stores the path and integrates; the threaded executor's workers now pull chunks sized per run, so small images no longer idle threads.

First calls are 12 to 18 s for both packages on a leg that compiles its program (BlackLightPlus 23 s on the adaptive leg; the reference harness compiles it in a setup run of 32.6 s); on the CPU the reference's first calls are longer (23 against 6.5 s for native blocks I 384² at 128 threads). No two-device pair of either package has been recorded; the Slurm history of the record lists the attempts.

5. Running on anta

The host is its own Slurm node. Outside an allocation the devices are not accessible (nvidia-smi fails to initialise NVML); inside one they are:

srun -p preempt --gres=gpu:1 -c 2 -t 01:00:00 <command>      # at most 1 h and 2 CPUs per GPU

Jobs on preempt are cancelled without warning by other users' priority and long jobs, so long runs are split into short, resumable jobs and resubmitted; this machine's job scripts, runner and logs are under results/blp/gpu/ and bench/slurm/.

CUDA.jl's runtime JLL, compiled where no driver is visible, selects no runtime; the environments that load CUDA therefore pin it in a LocalPreferences.toml ([CUDA_Runtime_jll] version = "13.3", with CUDA_Runtime_jll in the project's [extras]): BlackLightPlus/, test/oracle/ and the test environment results/blp/gpu/env (BlackLightPlus developed, CUDA a direct dependency).
