Execution module

BlackLightPlus.Execution — Module
Execution

Where programs run: devices, the Executor protocol with its threaded and pooled implementations, the CPU/GPU switch, the one kernel, residency of host objects on devices, ray sources, scheduling, the image-block worklist, the exact accumulators, the cascade of the Monte Carlo layers and the device wavefront of a Monte Carlo phase. A program is an isbits-after-Adapt functor called per item as prog(item, i, outs...); every executor gives every item the same result. CUDA methods of the device protocol live in the CUDA extension.

BlackLightPlus.Execution.ChildBuffer — Type
ChildBuffer(T, capacity, layout::MCStreamLayout)

Scattered children of one layer of the layered scattering loop, as a structure of arrays: photons::Vector{T} and their streams rngs::Vector{MCRng}, capacity slots, count[1], the atomic slot counter of spawn attempts, and the stream layout of the run, from which child_stream names a child's stream by its genealogy. spawn! takes the next slot from the counter and writes the child and its stream if the slot exists; a child beyond the capacity is counted and dropped, and overflow reports how many. Children are therefore stored in arrival order, which is not deterministic; their set is.

Policy (the driver's): a layer whose buffer overflowed is rerun from the same inputs with a buffer of capacity at least count[1] (the rerun spawns the identical children, their streams being genealogy-keyed), so no child is ever lost. Read the children with children (copies, safe to hand to the next layer's program) and reset the buffer with empty!. Inside a pool program each lane writes a device buffer of its own of the same capacity; gather! appends the stored children of every lane's buffer and adds the attempt counts. In a program run by foreach_cascade! the buffer is the slot that the cascade fills per chunk and generation: only its element type and its layout are read.

BlackLightPlus.Execution.DevicePool — Type
DevicePool(devices; lanes = 2, chunk = :auto)

Persistent multi-device runtime over devices (device handles or CUDA ordinals): lanes worker tasks per device, spawned once, a shared work queue, one residency cache per device (resident) and the child slots each device has learned (learned_slots). The lanes of a device share the residency cache's arrays, which they only read, and own every array they write, so their launches overlap (share_lanes!); every job ends with sync_lanes on the devices of its lanes. chunk is the chunk setting of the executors built on the pool with PoolExecutor(pool): a positive item count, or :auto, sized per run (chunk_for). Every device must be functional (BackendUnavailable otherwise). Run Julia with more threads than devices so the workers of different devices run concurrently. close(pool) stops the workers and releases their buffers; a pool built here is the caller's to close, a pool obtained through PoolExecutor(devices) or resolve belongs to the process registry and is closed at exit.

BlackLightPlus.Execution.ExactBins — Type
ExactBins(bands::Bands; nu_min = 1e8, nu_max = 1e16, n_ebins = 200)

Exact spectral accumulator of escaped superphotons: per (row of bands, frequency bin) the photon count and the integer limbs of Σ w, Σ w E and Σ (w E)² (E in mₑc²), the last for the variance of the bin. Frequency bin i covers ln ν ∈ lnu_min + [(i − 1), i) dlnu with dlnu = ln(nu_max/nu_min)/n_ebins, ν = E mₑc²/h; a photon outside the grid is dropped. record! adds through atomics on host and device arrays alike, so the bins hold the exact sums whatever the order of arrival; merge! adds bins of the same grid by integer addition; Spectrum rounds them once per cell. Inside a pool program each lane's device form is a zeroed accumulator of its own, gathered back by gather!; with any other Adapt adaptor the arrays are adapted.

BlackLightPlus.Execution.PoolExecutor — Type
PoolExecutor(devices; lanes = 2, chunk = :auto)
PoolExecutor(pool::DevicePool; chunk = pool.chunk)

Runs a program on the devices of a DevicePool: items are cut into chunks of chunk items (:auto: sized per run, chunk_for), the chunks are pulled by the pool's lanes (lanes per device), and each chunk is one launch of the lane's device form of the program (ProgramTwins), its outputs copied back into the host arrays. The launches of the lanes of a device overlap: they read the same residency arrays (share_lanes!) and write lane-owned outputs and accumulators. devices are device handles or CUDA ordinals; executors built with the same devices and lanes share one registered pool, its residency caches and its learned child slots, whatever their chunk. Executors compare equal when they share the pool and the chunk setting.

WaveQueues()

BlackLightPlus.Execution.Wavefront — Type
Wavefront(; start, advance, retire, primary, layer, branch = nothing, key = nothing,
          doubling_cap = typemax(Int))

The programs of one Monte Carlo transport for foreach_wavefront!: the stage programs start (item g, a primary, placed into the cohort by place!), advance (member j of the cohort: at most K loop iterations, then admit! into B while live or into D once stopped, fault! for a state that broke the contract; its children go to the ChildBuffer it holds), retire (slot j of D: its record and tally) and branch (child j of C: its state opened and handed to admit_child!; nothing when the wavefront serves only calls without children), each called as prog(item, i) with a WaveQueues placeholder that every launch binds; the key stage key (member j of the cohort: its key stored and counted by count_key! into a KeyCount placeholder; nothing when the cohort is never sorted); and the history programs primary and layer of the same transport, which the cascade runs where the wavefront does not serve. doubling_cap is the most loop iterations the budget doubles to; a call with children caps it further at WAVE_SPAWN_CAP. The stage programs must agree on wave_state; the advance answers wave_steps, the key stage wave_keys.

BlackLightPlus.Execution.cost_order — Method
cost_order(curve, fov, npix; tile = 16) -> Vector{Int}

Queue order of a full npix × npix frame, longest predicted work first: the pixels grouped in tile × tile image tiles (column-major order inside a tile, which keeps the sampling locality of neighbouring rays), the tiles in descending predicted cost. A ray marches longest near the critical curve, where its orbit count grows as the logarithm of the inverse impact-parameter offset, so a pixel's predicted cost is log(n / (d + 1/4)) with d its distance to curve on a grid of max(1, tile ÷ 4)-pixel cells and n the grid size. Tiles of equal cost keep their index order.

BlackLightPlus.Execution.foreach_cascade! — Method
foreach_cascade!(exec, primary, layer, n, depth; capacity = nothing,
                 chunk = cascade_chunk(exec, n)) -> Int

Run a cascade of at most depth + 1 generations on the executor exec: generation 0 is the program primary over the items 1:n, generation g ≥ 1 the program layer over the children that generation g − 1 spawned. The items are cut into chunks of chunk consecutive items, and one worker runs every generation of a chunk before it takes the next chunk. Both programs are called as prog(item, i) without outputs: the primary's item is its global index, the layer's item its member of the Generation the layer program holds, which the cascade replaces by the chunk's children of the previous generation. A program spawns into the ChildBuffer it holds, a slot whose element type and layout the cascade reads (pass one of capacity 0) and which it replaces by the chunk's child buffer. A primary that holds no ChildBuffer spawns nothing, and only generation 0 runs. Returns the number of children spawned in all generations; the children of generation depth are counted, not tracked.

The programs' ExactBins and ExactScalars receive every addition exactly once; call gather! on them afterwards, as after foreach_item!. Children that overflow a buffer are recovered by running their parents again, after the buffer grows, into zeroed scratch accumulators of the same types, so a rerun neither counts twice nor changes the compiled program. On a PoolExecutor each lane passes the chunk's range to the device without staging, keeps the chunk's children in two lane-resident buffers swapped per generation and reads back only their count; a generation that can spawn records into lane scratch, added into the device's totals once its children fit, and an overflowing generation runs again in full with slots for its counted children and an eighth more, a rerun that describe counts. Every non-final device generation of every chunk starts with capacity child slots, or without one with child_slots: at least MIN_CHILD_SLOTS, half the chunk, the most children a non-final generation on the device has spawned so far, and the slots an eighth of the device's free memory holds, at most BUDGET_SLOTS_PER_PRIMARY per primary. On a ThreadedExecutor worker tasks pull the chunks and run their items in order into the shared totals, with two task-local buffers of capacity slots (nothing: MIN_CHILD_SLOTS) that grow; only an item whose children overflow runs again.

Determinism: the accumulated totals, the set of children and the returned count are bitwise independent of the executor, the chunking, the device count, the lane and the arrival order.

BlackLightPlus.Execution.foreach_item! — Method
foreach_item!(exec, prog, items; outs::Tuple = (), order = nothing) -> outs
foreach_item!(exec, (prog1 => items1, prog2 => items2, ...); outs::Tuple = ()) -> outs

Run the program prog once for every item of items (distinct integers) on the executor exec, as prog(item, i, outs...): the program writes its outputs for item at outs[k][i] and returns nothing. i is the slot that the executor gathers into outs[k][item] (the item itself under ThreadedExecutor, the position in the chunk on a device), so after the call outs[k][item] holds the item's outputs. A program without outputs (outs = ()) may write through atomics. order, a permutation of 1:length(items), sets the visiting order to items[order]. The second form queues several programs over their own items in one queue, part by part, sharing outs.

Determinism: bitwise per item, whatever the executor, chunk size, device count, lane scheduling or order.

BlackLightPlus.Execution.foreach_wavefront! — Method
foreach_wavefront!(exec, wf::Wavefront, n, depth; capacity = nothing,
                   chunk = nothing) -> Int

Run the transport of wf over the primaries 1:n with at most depth scattering generations, as foreach_cascade!(exec, wf.primary, wf.layer, n, depth) would, and return the children spawned. The device wavefront serves the call when exec is a PoolExecutor whose every device answers wavefront(dev) = true, the settings allow it (with_wavefront_settings) and the call is a phase the wavefront has stages for: depth = 0 (the children of the primaries counted and not tracked), or any depth when wf has a branch. Any other call runs the cascade with capacity and, when given, chunk (the cascade's default otherwise).

On the wavefront each device runs its own cohort on its first lane until no primary block remains and its cohort is empty. Per iteration: with a key stage and sort_every = m ≥ 1, before every m-th advance of the device (from its first) the cohort's read order sorted by key: the key launch, one copy of the counts to the host, their exclusive scan, one copy back and a KeyPlace launch; the counter block zeroed; the advance over the cohort in that order, at most K loop iterations per state, its children into the child queue C of cap_C slots; one read of the counter block, each of a fault, live + done ≠ nA and a queue beyond its slots an error; with more children than cap_C, C grows to the children and an eighth more, the executor counts a rerun and the advance runs again on the unchanged cohort; the retire launch over the stopped states; the branch over the children, each opened into the cohort or, beyond generation depth, counted as dropped, then a second read of the counter block; the refill: when fewer than P states are live and blocks remain, the device takes max(1, min(cld(P − nB, R), cld(r, ndev))) of the r remaining blocks of R consecutive primaries in one claim, in index order or, with cost_order, in decreasing block_costs of the start program, and runs the start over them, one launch per run of consecutive blocks; then the queues swap. With wait off the launches of an iteration are queued in this order on the lane's queue, the counter block is zeroed by a launch and copied by a queued copy into one of two pinned stages that alternate, and the host waits only before it reads the counter block or the sort's counts; launch ranges and settings are kernel arguments. Before its first refill a device waits, at most WAVE_ARRIVAL seconds, until every device of the call has reached its own, so the blocks of a call spread over every device. cap_C is capacity at every iteration when given; otherwise it starts at the larger of P and the child slots that earlier calls left on the device's first lane, and keeps every growth. A call with depth = 0 stores no child: the advance counts the children, all dropped. With doubling on, K doubles every iteration once no block remains and fewer than P/4 states are live, up to the phase's cap (at most WAVE_SPAWN_CAP when depth > 0). A device whose cohort retires nothing in M + 2 consecutive iterations (M = wave_steps of the advance) is an error. At the end the started primaries must equal n, their ranges must partition 1:n and the retired states must equal n plus the children spawned less those dropped. In finally the child slot of every program is emptied.

Determinism: the accumulated totals and the returned count are those of the cascade, bitwise when the stage programs give the history programs' bits, whatever the settings, the child slots, the device count and the arrival order.

BlackLightPlus.Execution.stream_blocks! — Method

Run the refinement worklist: march every ImageBlock of work and of the children that visits return, until none remain. A chunk of blocks (at most max(1, c ÷ block²) of them, c the executor's chunk for the pixels of work, resolve_chunk!) is one run of the program prog over the chunk's block × block pixels, prog seeing its BlockGrid filled with the chunk's blocks (so prog must reach that grid through Adapt.adapt_structure). Each item writes one value of type outtype. Then visit(blk, vals) is called for each block of the chunk, vals[(i - 1) * block + j] holding pixel (i, j) at (a0 + (i - 0.5) ps, b0 + (j - 0.5) ps), ps = span / block; it returns the block's children (any iterable of ImageBlock), which are queued at once. visit may run concurrently for different blocks, may keep vals, and must not start executor work.

Determinism: the visited blocks and each block's values are bitwise independent of the executor, the chunking, the device count and the completion order.

BlackLightPlus.Execution.lane_check! — Method
lane_check!(on::Bool) -> Bool

The lane check, a debug switch, off by default: while on, every gather! of accumulator or child twins, which frees their device arrays without waiting for the lanes, first asserts that the queue of every lane of each twin's device is idle and throws an ErrorException naming the busy lane otherwise. An eviction from a residency cache waits for every lane of the device (sync_lanes) before it frees and is not checked; a lane frees its own buffers while the other lanes run. Returns the previous setting; lane_check() reads it.

BlackLightPlus.Execution.lane_sharing! — Method
lane_sharing!(on::Bool) -> Bool

Whether the residency cache shares the device arrays it uploads from now on between the lanes of their device (share_lanes!); on by default.
