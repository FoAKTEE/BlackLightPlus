BlackLightPlus.Products — Module
Products

The products: programs built from a plan and the entry points that run them on an executor chosen by the backend keyword.

source

Public

BlackLightPlus.Products.LEDGER_STRIPES — Constant

Stripes of the Monte Carlo ledger: the program of item g adds to stripe (g − 1) mod LEDGER_STRIPES, so the atomics of one quantity spread over that many cells.

BlackLightPlus.Products.TRACE_WINDOW — Constant

Steps a trace window holds. A window's outputs are TRACE_WINDOW + 2 arrays, which keeps the program call under the argument count at which Julia boxes its arguments.

BlackLightPlus.Products.AdaptiveTraceProgram — Type
AdaptiveTraceProgram(st, rays, escape)

Host only. The adaptive trajectory of one camera ray: trace_geodesic(st, y0; escape, reltol = 1e-9, abstol = 1e-11, λmax = 1e5) with every step saved, stored source → camera in states[i] with dls[i][k] the affine span from state k to k + 1 (last 0). Throws when the camera ray has no positive-energy null root.

BlackLightPlus.Products.AnalyticProgram — Type
AnalyticProgram(gen, theta_o, a, ro, disk, bands)

One screen point of the exact-Kerr equatorial tracer: (α, β) = image_coords(gen, item) (Bardeen coordinates), prep = pixel_prep(α, β, theta_o, a; ro) once, then for each photon-ring order n of bands (an NTuple{K,Int}) the landing band_landing(prep, n) and the intensity intensity(disk, landing). Called as prog(item, i, outs...) with the 5K Float64 vectors of analytic_outputs: outs[(q - 1) K + b][i] is quantity q of band b, the quantities being the band intensity, the landing radius, the time of flight, the azimuth and the emitted radial-velocity sign; an invalid landing gives intensity 0 and NaN observables. Needs a 32 KiB device stack.

BlackLightPlus.Products.GRMHDSpectrumResult — Type
GRMHDSpectrumResult

The spectrum of a plasma grid on a spacetime (mc_spectrum). spectra[k] is the one-row escaped Spectrum of the photons whose asymptotic polar angle falls in the inclination band incl_deg[k] ± half_width_deg (degrees), of solid angle dOmega[k] (nuLnu_solid(spectra[k], dOmega[k]), or incl_nuLnu, gives the isotropic-equivalent ν L_ν); total is the all-sky spectrum. Diagnostics: emitted_power and escaped_power (mₑc² Σ w E over the primaries at emission and over the escaped photons, erg s⁻¹), n_zones emitting zones, n_gen primaries, n_recorded escaped photons, and scattering, whether Compton scattering was tracked. plan and run as in Image.

source
BlackLightPlus.Products.Image — Type
Image

A rendered image: the Stokes planes I, Q, U, V (Matrix{Float64}, cgs specific intensity ν_obs³ times the invariant, laid out [iβ, iα] with β along the first axis; Q = U = V = 0 for StokesI), the plan it was rendered from and run, the record of the run: executor (describe of the executor), lanes (the plan's precision lanes), version (the package version) and seconds (wall time). A pixel whose camera ray has no positive-energy null root (a camera inside an ergoregion) is NaN.

BlackLightPlus.Products.ImagingProgram — Type
ImagingProgram(mode, st, control, provider, params, frame, rays)

One pixel of an image: item item gets its camera ray from rays (a CameraRays source, made in Float64 on the transfer spacetime), narrowed to the lane of control (a SpacetimeMarch on the geodesic lane), and out[i] = march(mode, y0, prog) reads st (the Float64 spacetime of the transfer), control, provider, params and frame. A ray without a positive-energy null root at the camera gets invalid. Workgroup size 64 for IQUV (register-limited occupancy), the backend's choice for StokesI.

MCLayer(generation::Generation, transport, sink)

One scattered child of a layer of the layered scattering loop, called as prog(item, i) without outputs: item j tracks photons[j] on its genealogy stream rngs[j] (the members of the Execution.Generation generation, which foreach_cascade! fills) by the MCTransport transport into the MCSink sink (ledger stripe of item j).

BlackLightPlus.Products.MCPrimary — Type
MCPrimary(source, transport, sink, seed)

One primary superphoton of a Monte Carlo run, called as prog(item, i) without outputs: item g draws the stream primary_rng(seed, g), is emitted by source (SphereSource, ZoneSource), adds its w E to the emitted power of the ledger and is tracked by the MCTransport transport into the MCSink sink (ledger stripe of item g).

BlackLightPlus.Products.MCSink — Type
MCSink(ctl, orders, children, ledger[, stripe = 0])

The sink of the Monte Carlo programs over the exact accumulators: an escaped photon of scattering order n is recorded into orders[min(n, K) + 1] (orders a tuple of K + 1 ExactBins) with the direction escape_direction(ctl, sph) and its w E into the escaped power of ledger (a mc_ledger); a child goes to the ChildBuffer children (nothing without scattering); the scattering ledger of tally! goes to the first four quantities of ledger. Every addition goes to stripe stripe. isbits after Adapt.adapt; writes through atomics only.

BlackLightPlus.Products.MCTransport — Type
MCTransport(ctl, st, provider, opac, absorb, scatter)

The fixed inputs of every march of a Monte Carlo run: the control ctl (FlatMarch or CurvedMarch), the spacetime st, the plasma provider, the Opacities opac and the compile-time switches absorb and scatter (Vals) of track!.

BlackLightPlus.Products.ScatterResult — Type
ScatterResult

The spectrum of the scattering sphere (mc_spectrum with scattering = true). orders[n + 1] is the escaped Spectrum of the photons of scattering order n (orders[end] holds the order max_scatt), total the exact sum of the orders. Diagnostics: n_primary primaries and n_children children tracked, the weight of the primaries w_primary, the weight spawned into children w_children, of which w_first_order by primaries, and scatter_loss, the incident weight lost to scattering (w_children ≈ scatter_loss checks the unbiasedness of the exponential transform). plan and run as in Image.

source
BlackLightPlus.Products.ShadowProgram — Type
ShadowProgram(st, budget, escape, rays)

The fate of one camera ray without plasma, on the geodesic lane: at every step the null error |g^{μν} p_μ p_ν| is recorded (its finite maximum kept), then the ray is captured when march_control(st, y, …, budget) stops (budget has infinite escape and emission radii, so it stops on capture only), escapes when the decision's radius exceeds escape (no outbound test), and is otherwise stepped by rk4_step; a non-finite state after a step counts as captured. out_y[i] is the final state and out_m[i] = (fate, steps, max null error, final coordinate radius) with fate 1 captured, 2 escaped, 0 step-limited, -1 no positive-energy null root at the camera.

BlackLightPlus.Products.StoredPathProgram — Type
StoredPathProgram(mode, st, provider, params, frame, states, dls)

Host only. One pixel marched along its stored trajectory: out[i] = march(mode, states[item][end], prog) under the control StoredPath(st, states[item], dls[item]) (states source → camera, as Trajectories holds them). Holds the trajectory vectors, so it is not a device program.

BlackLightPlus.Products.TraceProgram — Type
TraceProgram(st, budget, rays, resume)

One window of a fixed-control trace on the geodesic lane: march_control(st, …, budget) decides each step and rk4_step takes it, for at most TRACE_WINDOW steps and at most budget.nmax in all. The window starts at the camera ray of rays (narrowed to the lane) when resume === nothing, else at resume[item], a TraceState. Called as prog(item, i, w_0, …, w_K, state) with K = TRACE_WINDOW: w_0[i] is the start state, w_s[i] the TraceStep s of the window, state[i] the ray's TraceState after the window (slots past the window's last step are not written). Successive windows continue the march exactly, so the trajectory is the one a single march takes.

Where a traced ray stands after a window: state y, the radius r_prev of the last march decision, done steps taken in all, live (neither stopped nor at the step limit) and ok (the camera ray had a positive-energy null root).

BlackLightPlus.Products.emission_zones — Method
emission_zones(plan; stride = 1, r_emit_max = Inf, max_zones = typemax(Int))
    -> Vector{EmissionZone{Float64}}
emission_zones(blocks::FluidBlocks, st, params; stride, r_emit_max, max_zones)

One emission zone per cell centre of the plan's matter (an AthenaKSnapshot or FluidBlocks; every stride-th cell per axis) that its own block owns (locate, so a cell under a finer block is skipped), within Kerr–Schild radius r_emit_max, whose plasma decoded on the plan's spacetime with the plan's parameter set is valid with n_e > 0 and Θₑ > 0. The zone's volume is the proper volume √−g Δx¹Δx²Δx³ stride³ L_unit³ (cm³), √−g taken as 1 where det g ≥ 0. Cells are visited block by block, k, j, i outermost to innermost; at most max_zones zones.

BlackLightPlus.Products.mc_spectrum — Method
mc_spectrum(plan, Ns; scattering = false, backend = nothing, bands = nothing,
            seed = 0x5eed5eed, nu_min = 1e8, nu_max = 1e16, spec_nu_min = nu_min,
            spec_nu_max = scattering ? 1e24 : nu_max, n_esamp = 400, n_ebins = 200,
            tabulated = false, table = nothing, max_scatt = 3, bias = nothing,
            absorption, capacity = nothing, C = plan.budget.C, escape = 1e3,
            R_em = 100.0, stride = 1, r_emit_max = Inf, max_zones = typemax(Int))
    -> Spectrum | ScatterResult | GRMHDSpectrumResult

The Monte Carlo spectrum of the plan's matter from about Ns superphotons, run on the executor resolve(backend) (nothing: the session default).

The matter decides the run. The uniform sphere of prepare(sphere) (flat spacetime): primaries uniform in the ball, a straight-line march (FlatMarch); without scattering the result is the absorbed Spectrum, with scattering a ScatterResult of per-order spectra (absorption defaults to false there: the idealized Comptonization sphere). A plasma grid (AthenaKSnapshot, FluidBlocks) on a stationary spacetime: primaries spread over the emission_zones of the plan (stride, r_emit_max, max_zones) by their expected counts, rounded stochastically on the stream MCRng(seed, 0); a geodesic march (CurvedMarch: step divisor C, escape at radius escape, emission-confinement radius the larger of R_em and 1.2 times the outermost zone radius); absorption always by default; the result a GRMHDSpectrumResult.

Generation: the weight table over [nu_min, nu_max] on n_esamp intervals for about Ns primaries, exact (tabulated = false) or tabulated frequency rejection. Primary g draws the stream primary_rng(seed, g). Transport: absorption (absorption), Compton scattering (scattering) with the hot-cross table (nothing: hotcross_table(plan), built at the plan's first scattering run and kept in the plan), at most max_scatt scatterings per photon, the importance bias (a Bias, its b0, or nothing: the sphere's Thomson depth, 1 on a grid), lengths in plan.params.units.L_unit cm (one cm for a plan without parameters). Binning: bands (PolarBands(6) for the sphere, InclinationBands((17, 50, 90), 10) on a grid, which takes inclination bands only), n_ebins log-frequency bins over [spec_nu_min, spec_nu_max].

Scattering runs as a cascade over chunks of primaries (Execution.foreach_cascade!): within a chunk, each layer's children are tracked by the next layer, up to max_scatt layers, and stay on the worker that runs the chunk. On devices a layer starts with a child buffer of capacity slots (nothing: at least 4096, half the chunk's primaries); a layer whose buffer overflows is rerun into zeroed scratch accumulators with a buffer of the counted size, so no child is lost or counted twice. On threads each task's two child buffers start with capacity slots (nothing: 4096) and grow; a photon whose children overflow is rerun the same way.

Determinism: bitwise reduction. The bins, the ledger and the children depend only on the plan, Ns and the keywords, whatever the executor, the device count, the chunking or the buffer capacity.

BlackLightPlus.Products.photon_ring_layers — Method
photon_ring_layers(plan, disk::EquatorialDisk; bands = (0, 1, 2), dx = 0.02, box = 12.0,
                   npts = 100, ro = plan.camera.d, fudge = 1.0, backend = nothing)
    -> NamedTuple

The layered photon-ring image of the equatorial disk on the Bardeen screen: the grid of spacing dx over [-box, box]² of lensing band 0 (band_grid), and for each order n ∈ bands its own lensing band (lensing_band(a, θo, n; npts, box)), whose in-band grid points alone are traced to their order-n landing and painted with the disk intensity (AnalyticProgram over the band's points). Confining each order to its band keeps the n ≥ 1 rings thin. The plan's spacetime must be Kerr with the disk's spin. Returns (I, Ib, masks, grid, N, box, dx, bands, plan, run): per band the N × N image Ib[b] (rows β) and masks[b] (painted and positive), the composite I = Σ_b w_b Ib[b] with w = 1 for n = 0 and fudge otherwise, and the shared grid.

Determinism: bitwise per item, whatever the executor, device count or chunking.

BlackLightPlus.Products.render — Method
render(plan; backend = nothing) -> Image
render(plan; path::Trajectories, backend = nothing) -> Image

The image of plan: every pixel's camera ray marched with the fused fixed-control march of plan.mode (geodesic, plasma sample and transfer in one pass), queued in plan.queue order on the executor resolve(backend) (nothing: the session default). Under MixedLanes the rescue pixels are marched on the Float64 lane, queued first, and the rest on the Float32 lane, in one queue. With path, a trace of the same plan (trace), each pixel is marched along its stored trajectory instead (the two-phase path; host only: backend must resolve to a ThreadedExecutor).

Determinism: bitwise per item, whatever the executor, device count, chunking or queue order.

BlackLightPlus.Products.render_amr — Method
render_amr(plan; block = 16, max_level = 2, refine = refinement_indicator(),
           backend = nothing) -> NamedTuple

Adaptive-refinement image of plan: the camera frame is tiled into block × block pixel blocks (the resolution must be a multiple of block), every block is marched with the plan's imaging program on its geodesic lane (ImagingProgram over a BlockGrid), and a block whose Stokes I values I[i, j] (pixel (i, j) at (a0 + (i - ½) ps, b0 + (j - ½) ps)) satisfy needs_refinement(refine, I) is replaced by four children of half its side at the same pixel count, down to level max_level. refine is a refinement indicator of Analysis or any callable f(I) -> Bool. Blocks stream through stream_blocks! on the executor resolve(backend): children are marched as soon as their parent is tested. Returns (image, levels, nrays): image an Image mosaic at resolution × 2^max_level per side (each pixel the value of the finest block covering it), levels[β block, α block] the finest level reached in each base block, nrays the rays marched. MixedLanes plans are an ArgumentError.

Determinism: bitwise per item; the visited blocks, the mosaic, the level map and the ray count are independent of the executor, the chunking and the completion order.

BlackLightPlus.Products.render_analytic — Method
render_analytic(plan, disk::EquatorialDisk; bands = (0, 1, 2), ro = plan.camera.d,
                backend = nothing) -> NamedTuple

The exact-Kerr image of the equatorial disk disk on the plan's pixels: for every pixel (its image coordinates are Bardeen's (α, β)) and every photon-ring order n ∈ bands, the closed-form equatorial landing (pixel_prep once per pixel with the observer at inclination plan.theta_o and radius ro, then band_landing) and the disk's observed intensity there (AnalyticProgram). The plan's spacetime must be Kerr with the disk's spin; its matter is not used. Returns (I, Ib, rs, ts, phis, redshift_sign, bands, plan, run): per band b the image Ib[b] and the landing radius rs[b], time of flight ts[b], azimuth phis[b] and emitted radial-velocity sign redshift_sign[b] (each npix × npix, NaN off the disk; Ib zero there), and I = Σ_b Ib[b], summed in band order. The pixels are visited in their index order, so every chunk of the executor writes a contiguous run of each map.

Determinism: bitwise per item, whatever the executor, device count or chunking.

BlackLightPlus.Products.sensitivity — Method
sensitivity(plan, param::Symbol; backend = nothing) -> NamedTuple

The Stokes I image of plan and its exact derivative with respect to param, by forward-mode differentiation of the transfer (the per-step transfer is affine in the intensity, so the derivative of the march is exact): param = :mass_scale seeds the density mass scale (cgs, in the units), param = :rat_high the β → ∞ ion-to-electron temperature ratio (in the electron model); anything else is an ArgumentError. Returns (image, derivative, flux, dflux, param): image an Image (Stokes I), derivative the npix × npix map ∂I/∂param, flux the sum of the image over the pixels in camera order and dflux its derivative. Runs on the plan's lanes, on the executor resolve(backend).

Determinism: bitwise per item, whatever the executor, device count or chunking.

BlackLightPlus.Products.shadow — Method
shadow(plan; backend = nothing) -> NamedTuple

The fate of every camera ray of plan without plasma (ShadowProgram) on the plan's geodesic lane: the step of the fixed-control march, capture by the spacetime's rule, escape once the march radius exceeds plan.escape, at most max_steps steps. Returns (fate, steps, null_error, radius, final, plan, run), each map npix × npix in the image layout: fate 1 captured, 2 escaped, 0 step-limited; steps taken; null_error the largest finite |g^{μν} p_μ p_ν| along the ray; radius and final the coordinate radius and state where the ray ended. MixedLanes plans are an ArgumentError. A camera ray without a positive-energy null root is an error.

Determinism: bitwise per item, whatever the executor, device count or chunking.

BlackLightPlus.Products.trace — Method
trace(plan; backend = nothing, adaptive = false) -> Trajectories

The trajectories of the plan's camera rays. By default the fixed-control march of the imaging products on the plan's geodesic lane (step (distance to the horizon proxy)/C, stop on capture, on outbound escape past escape or past R_em, at most max_steps steps), run window by window (TraceProgram) on the executor resolve(backend); states and steps of a Float32 lane are widened to Float64. With adaptive = true the host-only adaptive solve trace_geodesic (escape = plan.escape, reltol = 1e-9, abstol = 1e-11), which needs a ThreadedExecutor. MixedLanes plans are an ArgumentError: a trace runs on one lane. A camera ray without a positive-energy null root is an error.

Determinism: bitwise per item, whatever the executor, device count or chunking.

BlackLightPlus.Products.variants — Method
variants(plan, mass_scales; backend = nothing) -> NamedTuple

K = length(mass_scales) Stokes I images of plan in one pass, image k for the density mass scale mass_scales[k] (g): each ray is marched and its plasma sampled once, and the transfer runs once per mass scale on the shared samples (the parameter sets differ only in their units). Determinism: bitwise per item, whatever the executor, device count or chunking.
