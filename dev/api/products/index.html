BlackLightPlus.Products — Module
Products

The products: programs built from a plan and the entry points that run them on an executor chosen by the backend keyword.

source

Public

BlackLightPlus.Products.LEDGER_STRIPES — Constant

Stripes of the Monte Carlo ledger: the program of item g adds to stripe (g − 1) mod LEDGER_STRIPES, so the atomics of one quantity spread over that many cells.

BlackLightPlus.Products.TRACE_WINDOW — Constant

Steps a trace window holds. A window's outputs are TRACE_WINDOW + 2 arrays, which keeps the program call under the argument count at which Julia boxes its arguments.

BlackLightPlus.Products.AdaptiveTraceProgram — Type
AdaptiveTraceProgram(st, rays, escape)

Host only. The adaptive trajectory of one camera ray: trace_geodesic(st, y0; escape, reltol = 1e-9, abstol = 1e-11, λmax = 1e5) with every step saved, stored source → camera in states[i] with dls[i][k] the affine span from state k to k + 1 (last 0). Throws when the camera ray has no positive-energy null root.

BlackLightPlus.Products.AnalyticProgram — Type
AnalyticProgram(gen, theta_o, a, ro, disk, bands)

One screen point of the exact-Kerr equatorial tracer: (α, β) = image_coords(gen, item) (Bardeen coordinates), prep = pixel_prep(α, β, theta_o, a; ro) once, then for each photon-ring order n of bands (an NTuple{K,Int}) the landing band_landing(prep, n) and the intensity intensity(disk, landing). Called as prog(item, i, outs...) with the 5K Float64 vectors of analytic_outputs: outs[(q - 1) K + b][i] is quantity q of band b, the quantities being the band intensity, the landing radius, the time of flight, the azimuth and the emitted radial-velocity sign; an invalid landing gives intensity 0 and NaN observables. Needs a 32 KiB device stack.

source
BlackLightPlus.Products.BandSum — Type
BandSum(sums)

Band sums of per-band maps: sums holds, per output k, a tuple of band maps (m₁, …, m_K), and item item writes outs[k][i] = ((0 + m₁[item]) + m₂[item]) + … + m_K[item], the bands added in order starting from zero, which gives the bits of I = zeros(…) followed by I .+= m_b band after band. Called as prog(item, i, outs...) with one output per entry of sums. The maps of one sum share their size (else a DimensionMismatch), and an item outside them is a BoundsError.

source
BlackLightPlus.Products.CrossingProgram — Type
CrossingProgram(st, budget, rays, disk, orders)

One screen point of the marched image of the equatorial disk disk on the Kerr spacetime st (geodesic lane, budget its fixed-control budget): the camera ray of rays is advanced by march_step(budget.integrator, …) under march_control, and every step whose end lies on the other side of the plane z = 0 yields the crossing state surface_crossing(budget.integrator, st, y, h, EquatorialPlane()); a ninth-order march carries its state compensated (march_step_compensated). At crossing k the landing of order k − 1 is read off the state: the Kerr–Schild (= Boyer–Lindquist) radius r, the photon's λ = −p_φ/p_t and η = r² p_z²/p_t² (p_θ = −r p_z on the equator), the emitted radial direction νr = −sign(dr/dλ) of the backward ray, the Kerr–Schild time x^t and azimuth; the intensity is equatorial_disk_intensity(r, νr, a, λ, η) with the disk's knobs. The march stops on the budget's rule or after the highest order of orders (an NTuple{K,Int} of ascending orders from 0). Called as prog(item, i, outs...) with the 5K + 1 vectors of crossing_outputs: quantity-major as analytic_outputs (intensity, radius, time, azimuth, radial sign), then the step count. A missing crossing gives intensity 0 and NaN observables; a ray without a positive-energy null root at the camera gives NaN everywhere.

BlackLightPlus.Products.GRMHDSpectrumResult — Type
GRMHDSpectrumResult

The spectrum of a plasma grid on a spacetime (mc_spectrum). spectra[k] is the one-row escaped Spectrum of the photons whose asymptotic polar angle falls in the inclination band incl_deg[k] ± half_width_deg (degrees), of solid angle dOmega[k] (nuLnu_solid(spectra[k], dOmega[k]), or incl_nuLnu, gives the isotropic-equivalent ν L_ν); total is the all-sky spectrum; both sum the scattering orders, and orders[n + 1] is the spectrum of order n alone, every band and the all-sky row (orders[end] the order max_scatt; one order without scattering). Every spectrum carries Σ (w E)² per bin for its standard error (Execution.nuLnu_sigma). Diagnostics: n_zones emitting zones, n_gen primaries, n_recorded escaped photons, n_children children, scattering, whether Compton scattering was tracked; the weights w_primary, w_children, w_first_order, scatter_loss, scatter_loss_final and w_unspawned and the powers emitted_power, escaped_power, absorbed_power, captured_power and dropped_power of a ScatterResult (the weights zero without scattering). plan and run as in Image.

BlackLightPlus.Products.Image — Type
Image

A rendered image: the Stokes planes I, Q, U, V (Matrix{Float64}, cgs specific intensity ν_obs³ times the invariant, laid out [iβ, iα] with β along the first axis; Q = U = V = 0 for StokesI), the plan it was rendered from and run, the record of the run: executor (describe of the executor), lanes (the plan's precision lanes), version (the package version) and seconds (wall time). BlackLightPlus.Products.ImagingProgram — Type
ImagingProgram(mode, st, control, provider, params, frame, rays)

One pixel of an image: item item gets its camera ray from rays (a CameraRays source, made in Float64 on the transfer spacetime), narrowed to the lane of control (a SpacetimeMarch on the geodesic lane), and out[i] = march(mode, y0, prog) reads st (the Float64 spacetime of the transfer), control, provider, params and frame. BlackLightPlus.Products.MCAdvance — Type
MCAdvance(transport, sink)

The advance stage of a Monte Carlo wavefront, called as prog(item, i): member j = item of the launch's cohort runs march_steps of its march up to loop iteration min(it − 1 + min(K, M), M) (K the launch's budget, M = max_steps(ctl), in Int64) and is admitted to the live queue while its outcome is LIVE_MARCH, to the stopped queue otherwise (Execution.admit!); a live state whose iteration did not grow, or a stopped state without an outcome, counts a fault (Execution.fault!). Its children are kicked inside the march by the sink's branch! and go to the child queue the launch binds as the sink's ChildBuffer. It writes no accumulator.

The statements of MCLayer before its march.

BlackLightPlus.Products.MCKey — Type
MCKey(transport)

The key stage of a Monte Carlo wavefront, called as prog(item, i): member j = item of the launch's cohort is keyed by its scattering generation g (the entry photon's nscatt, at most the max_scatt of the MCTransport transport), then by photon_key of its state on the transport's spacetime under its march control: g × PHOTON_KEYS + photon_key, one of (max_scatt + 1) × PHOTON_KEYS values, stored and counted by Execution.count_key!. Sorted so, the warps of an advance hold photons of one generation with nearby positions and directions. The key orders the advance's reads of the cohort and enters no result.

BlackLightPlus.Products.ScatterResult — Type
ScatterResult

The spectrum of the scattering sphere (mc_spectrum with scattering = true). orders[n + 1] is the escaped Spectrum of the photons of scattering order n (orders[end] holds the order max_scatt), total the exact sum of the orders; each carries Σ (w E)² per bin for its standard error (Execution.nuLnu_sigma). BlackLightPlus.Products.ShadowProgram — Type
ShadowProgram(st, budget, escape, rays)

The fate of one camera ray without plasma, on the geodesic lane: at every step the null error |g^{μν} p_μ p_ν| is recorded (its finite maximum kept), then the ray is captured when march_control(st, y, …, budget) stops (budget has infinite escape and emission radii, so it stops on capture only), escapes when the decision's radius exceeds escape (no outbound test), and is otherwise stepped by the budget integrator's march_step; a non-finite state after a step counts as captured. out_y[i] is the final state and out_m[i] = (fate, steps, max null error, final coordinate radius) with fate 1 captured, 2 escaped, 0 step-limited, -1 no positive-energy null root at the camera.

BlackLightPlus.Products.TimedSpectrumResult — Type
TimedSpectrumResult

The Monte Carlo of a plasma over an emission window (mc_spectrum with window). result is what the stationary module returns for the same matter (a Spectrum, a ScatterResult or a GRMHDSpectrumResult) with every spectrum and power the mean over the window (t_a, t_b); rule the TimedBands of the time-resolved bins and orders[n + 1] their Spectrum for the photons of scattering order n (total the sum), at the arrival time t − n̂·x of a distant observer. interior is the span of arrival times reached by every emitting point over the whole window; outside it a light curve sees part of the source. shares are the nodes' shares W_m of the emission, omega the rotation rate of the emission and beyond_series the fraction of the march time, weighted by the photons' starting weights, spent after the last frame of a series or the last slice of an ADMSeries (0 for neither), where the last frame stands in for the plasma and the last slice for the metric; arrival_times, light_curve and time_spectrum read the bins.

BlackLightPlus.Products.TraceProgram — Type
TraceProgram(st, budget, rays, resume)

One window of a fixed-control trace on the geodesic lane: march_control(st, …, budget) decides each step and the budget integrator's march_step takes it (rk4_step for ClassicalRK4), for at most TRACE_WINDOW steps and at most budget.nmax in all. The window starts at the camera ray of rays (narrowed to the lane) when resume === nothing, else at resume[item], a TraceState. Called as prog(item, i, w_0, …, w_K, state) with K = TRACE_WINDOW: w_0[i] is the start state, w_s[i] the TraceStep s of the window, state[i] the ray's TraceState after the window (slots past the window's last step are not written). Successive windows continue the march exactly, so the trajectory is the one a single march takes.

BlackLightPlus.Products.analytic_movie — Method
analytic_movie(plan, spot::EquatorialSpot; times, bands = (0, 1, 2),
               ro = plan.camera.d, slow = true, backend = nothing, into = nothing)
    -> NamedTuple

Frames of the time-dependent equatorial source spot on the plan's pixels at the observation times times: the closed-form landings of every pixel and order once (SpotProgram; ro = Inf for the observer at infinity, whose times are retarded), then one FrameProgram per time. slow = false paints every landing at its observation time (fast light). Returns (frames, lightcurve, times, A, x, y, ts, bands, plan, run): frames[:, :, k] (rows β) the image at times[k], the light curve lightcurve[k] = Σ frames[:, :, k] × pixel area (M²), and the per-band landing maps. into, a result of this function with as many bands, pixels and times, is overwritten (its frames, light curve, times and landing maps) and shared by the returned result, bitwise the result without it; its arrays are dropped from the device pools' residency caches first (Execution.invalidate!). On a pool the frame programs read the landing maps from a copy on each device, uploaded every call (32K bytes per pixel and device, in host memory on a host device), with into as without it.

Determinism: bitwise per item, whatever the executor, device count or chunking.

BlackLightPlus.Products.mc_ledger — Method
mc_ledger() -> ExactScalars

The exact scalars of a Monte Carlo run: LEDGER_STRIPES stripes of eleven quantities. In weight (photons s⁻¹), from the scattering runs' MarchLedgers: the weight spawned into children, the weight spawned by primaries, the scattered weight of the steps that may spawn and of those that may not, the weight of the events that spawned nothing, and the weight of the primaries. BlackLightPlus.Products.mc_spectrum — Method
mc_spectrum(plan, Ns; scattering = false, backend = nothing, bands = nothing,
            seed = 0x5eed5eed, nu_min = 1e8, nu_max = 1e16, spec_nu_min = nu_min,
            spec_nu_max = scattering ? 1e24 : nu_max, n_esamp = 400, n_ebins = 200,
            table = nothing, max_scatt = 3, bias = nothing, bias_max = MC_BIAS_MAX,
            bias_order = 1, split = 1, absorption, capacity = nothing, C = plan.budget.C,
            escape = 1e3, R_em = 100.0, stride = 1, r_emit_max = Inf,
            max_zones = typemax(Int), medium = nothing, step_frac = SPHERE_STEP_FRAC,
            window = nothing, time_bins = 64, time_ebins = 20, sectors = 0)
    -> Spectrum | ScatterResult | GRMHDSpectrumResult | TimedSpectrumResult

The Monte Carlo spectrum of the plan's matter from about Ns superphotons, run on the executor resolve(backend) (nothing: the session default).

The matter decides the run. The uniform sphere of prepare(sphere) (flat spacetime): primaries uniform in the ball, a straight-line march (FlatMarch); without scattering the result is the absorbed Spectrum, with scattering a ScatterResult of per-order spectra (absorption defaults to false there: the idealized Comptonization sphere); medium is another plasma for the march (a SphericalShell about a small source: a light echo), whose radius extends the march, and step_frac the march step as a fraction of its radius. A plasma grid (AthenaKSnapshot, FluidBlocks) on any spacetime, a moving binary included: the grid is one snapshot, emitting from its cell centres at t = 0 and held fixed while the superphotons travel, and the march reads the metric at each photon's own time; primaries spread over the emission_zones of the plan (every owned cell above the horizon at stride = 1; one randomly drawn cell per group of stride³ with the group's volume otherwise, an unbiased quadrature for quick runs; r_emit_max, max_zones) by their expected counts, rounded stochastically on the stream MCRng(seed, 0); a geodesic march (CurvedMarch: step divisor C, escape at radius escape, emission-confinement radius the larger of R_em and 1.2 times the outermost zone radius); absorption always by default; the result a GRMHDSpectrumResult.

Generation: the weight table over [nu_min, nu_max] on n_esamp intervals for about Ns primaries (trapezoid counts, so their expectation is Ns), frequencies by rejection against ∫ j_ν dΩ from the process's EmissivityTable. Primary g draws the stream primary_rng(seed, g). Transport: absorption (absorption), Compton scattering (scattering) with the hot-cross table (nothing: hotcross_table(plan), the process's table, built on the run's executor at the process's first request), at most max_scatt scatterings per photon, every step's scattering events a Poisson process under the importance bias: nothing or a number b0 (1 by default) for the StateBias b = clamp(b0 A(Θₑ) (w/w_ref) bias_order^n, 1, bias_max) of a photon scattered n times, with w_ref the mean primary weight, bias_order the factor per scattering order (1: none; larger samples the light scattered two and three times and the high-frequency tail it makes) and split the most children per capped event (1: no splitting), or a Bias/StateBias used as given. Every choice leaves the spectrum unbiased; the bias and split set its variance and cost. Lengths in plan.params.units.L_unit cm (one cm for a plan without parameters). Binning: bands (PolarBands(6) for the sphere, InclinationBands((17, 50, 90), 10) on a grid, which takes inclination bands only), n_ebins log-frequency bins over [spec_nu_min, spec_nu_max].

On GPUs a stationary run, with or without scattering, runs on the device wavefront (Execution.foreach_wavefront!) with the bits of the cascade: each photon's march is cut into launches of at most a budget of loop iterations over a cohort that a block counter shared by the devices refills, and the children a launch spawns join the cohort of their device. The launch's child queue holds capacity slots (nothing: the cohort, or the larger queue an earlier call grew on the device), and a launch whose children overflow it runs again with a queue for the counted children and an eighth more. Elsewhere scattering runs as a cascade over chunks of primaries (Execution.foreach_cascade!): within a chunk, each layer's children are tracked by the next layer, up to max_scatt layers, and stay on the worker that runs the chunk. On the devices of a cascade a layer starts with a child buffer of capacity slots (nothing: at least 4096, half the chunk's primaries, the most children a layer on the device has spawned in the process, and the slots an eighth of the device's free memory holds, at most 16 per primary); a layer whose buffer overflows is rerun into zeroed scratch accumulators with a buffer for the counted children and an eighth more, so no child is lost or counted twice. On threads each task's two child buffers start with capacity slots (nothing: 4096) and grow; a photon whose children overflow is rerun the same way.

Emission over a window: with window = (t_a, t_b) (coordinate time) the plasma emits over the window and the result is a TimedSpectrumResult: the stationary result of the same matter, with every spectrum the window mean, and the escaped photons binned by their arrival time t − n̂·x at a distant observer into time_bins equal bins over [t_a − R, t_b + R] (R the march's emission radius; or (t_lo, t_hi, n)) with an underflow and an overflow bin, on time_ebins frequency bins over [spec_nu_min, spec_nu_max], in the run's bands split into sectors azimuth sectors (Execution.SectorBands; 0: none). The matter may be a frozen snapshot or sphere (on a stationary spacetime), a Data.Corotating plasma (on a spacetime with that helical symmetry), a Data.FrameSequence or SeriesProvider of frames (on a stationary spacetime; the frame times in the coordinate time of the spacetime, the window inside them), a Data.FrameStack (each frame emitting from its own time over its hat, carried at the stack's omega: on a stationary spacetime with omega = 0, or on a spacetime with the helical symmetry of omega, a moving binary, with the frames carried along its orbit; held in place, two frames or more, on a spacetime with no helical symmetry, an ADMSeries or a UserMetric of t, each photon generated at its own event (Products.EventSource); the march reads the stack at each photon's time) or a Matter.VariableSphere; the time-dependent ones need the window. The march reads plasma and metric at each photon's time. Runs on CPU threads.

Determinism: bitwise reduction. The bins, the ledger and the children depend only on the plan, Ns and the keywords, whatever the executor, the device count, the chunking or the buffer capacity. A frozen snapshot over a window is the stationary run bit for bit but for the photons' emission times.

BlackLightPlus.Products.photon_key — Method
photon_key(st, ctl, y::SVector{8,Float64}) -> Int

The order key (s × 8 + octant(x)) × 8 + octant(k) in 0:8191 of the state y (position x, covariant momentum p) on the spacetime st under the Monte Carlo march control ctl (FlatMarch or CurvedMarch). A total function:

  • s is the shell of the coordinate radius r = √(x² + y² + z²) above the floor r_min: horizon_floor(st) when that is positive and finite, otherwise the march radius of ctl (the R of a FlatMarch, min(R_em, escape) of a CurvedMarch) times e^−6.4. s = 0 when r is not finite or r ≤ r_min (or r_min is not positive), else unsafe_trunc(Int, min((log r − log r_min) / 0.05, 127.0));

  • a state with a non-finite entry of x, p or k has key 0.

The logarithm is tang_log and r and k are sums of plain products, so the shell and the position octant of a state are the same on the host and on a device; the direction octant follows each backend's rounding of the inverse metric. The key orders photons and enters no result.

The last run's zones are reused by a run on the same blocks (the same object, not changed in place since), spacetime, parameters and zone options (and seed, at a stride above 1), and its tables by a run that also has the same generation settings; both are what the run would build, bitwise. A run whose stop sphere leaves the lattice of an ADM spacetime is refused (check_stop_sphere).

BlackLightPlus.Products.source_costs — Method
source_costs(source, n, R) -> Union{Nothing,Vector{Float64}}

The cost of each block of R consecutive primaries of 1:n emitted by source, by which the refill of the device wavefront orders the blocks (Execution.block_costs). A ZoneSource: the mean of 1 / r over COST_SAMPLES primaries spread evenly over the block, r = √(x² + y² + z²) the coordinate radius of the zone that emits each (the zone emit draws from), so the refill takes the blocks of the inner zones first. The cost ranks the blocks by radius; it does not measure their photons' work. Every other source: nothing (index order). The costs order the refill and enter no result.

BlackLightPlus.Products.staged_planes! — Method
staged_planes!(exec::ThreadedExecutor, parts, P::StokesPlanes) -> P

The parts (prog => items, …) of an IQUV image on Julia threads into the planes P: the chunks that foreach_item! cuts, each marched in blocks of at most STAGE_ITEMS items into the worker's own vector of Stokes vectors, whose components are then stored in the planes. On threads a program writes its output itself and is compiled for the output's type, and a program compiled for StokesPlanes contracts its arithmetic otherwise; the worker's vector has the type of the output of the call without into, so the same compiled program runs and the planes hold that call's bits.
