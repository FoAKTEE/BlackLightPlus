Spacetime module

BlackLightPlus.Spacetime — Module
Spacetime
Spacetime{T}

The module: spacetimes and null geodesics — the Spacetime protocol (metric, inverse, index operations, gradients), the geodesic and parallel-transport right-hand sides, the fixed-step integrator, the termination rule, the camera, Kerr orbit primitives and the analytic Kerr lensing bands.

The abstract type: a metric on Cartesian coordinates x^μ = (t, x, y, z) with signature (−,+,+,+) in geometrized units. T is the number type of the spacetime's own scalars (the lane: Float64, Float32, or a ForwardDiff.Dual for parameter derivatives). A concrete spacetime implements metric; the index operations, the gradients, the geodesic right-hand sides and the integrator then follow.

BlackLightPlus.Spacetime.ADMGrid — Type
ADMGrid{T,A<:AbstractArray{T,4}} <: StationarySpacetime{T}
ADMGrid(xs, ys, zs, data; alpha_min = 0.1, offgrid_eta = true)
ADMGrid(st::ADMGrid; alpha_min = st.alpha_min)

Frozen-slice ADM data on a regular Cartesian grid. data[c, i, j, k] holds the ten fields per node in the order (α, β^x, β^y, β^z, γ_xx, γ_xy, γ_xz, γ_yy, γ_yz, γ_zz); the node coordinates are x0 + (i-1) dx (likewise y, z), with nx, ny, nz nodes per axis. The metric is assembled from the Catmull-Rom interpolants: g_00 = -α² + β_i β^i, g_0i = β_i = γ_ij β^j, g_ij = γ_ij. Off the grid the metric is Minkowski when offgrid_eta, else the clamped extrapolation. The march captures where the interpolated lapse drops below alpha_min. Adapt.adapt(backend, st) moves data to a device; the second constructor copies the grid with another capture lapse, sharing data. The scalars share the type of the data, so lane(st, T) returns st.

BlackLightPlus.Spacetime.BoostedSuperposedKerrSchild — Type
BoostedSuperposedKerrSchild{N,T,F} <: Spacetime{T}
BoostedSuperposedKerrSchild(masses, spins, trajectory; boost = true)

N Kerr-Schild holes in prescribed motion, g_μν = η_μν + Σ_A f_A l^A_μ l^A_ν with each hole's term evaluated in the rest frame of its velocity: at the offset x'_A = Λ(v_A)(x - s_A(t)) (the spatial Lorentz contraction) and with the covector boosted back, l^A = Λ(v_A) l'^A. Fields: mass and spin (a spin vector a_A, |a_A| ≤ M_A) per hole, trajectory, the callable trajectory(t) -> NTuple{N} of (s_A, v_A) pairs (a CircularOrbit or any closure generic in t), boost, and r_h, the per-hole horizon radii M_A + √(M_A² - |a_A|²) measured in x'_A. With boost = false the holes move without the boost of their terms (the static superposition carried along the trajectory). The vector constructor builds the Float64 lane; lane(st, T) converts the scalars and a CircularOrbit.

Landing{T}

BlackLightPlus.Spacetime.adm_grid_from_metric — Method
adm_grid_from_metric(metric_fn, xs, ys, zs; alpha_min = 0.1, offgrid_eta = true,
                     excise = nothing) -> ADMGrid

Sample a covariant metric closure metric_fn(x::SVector{4}) -> SMatrix{4,4} on the t = 0 slice at the nodes of the ranges xs, ys, zs and store its ADM data. Nodes where excise(x) is true, or where the metric is non-finite, take the collapsed-lapse fill α = alpha_min/2, β = 0, γ = I: invertible, and below the capture lapse, so a ray falling into the excised set stops on the lapse. Pass the horizon predicate (e.g. x -> ks_radius(st, x) < horizon_radius(st)) for an inner stopping surface independent of the grid alignment. Serial; the values do not depend on the traversal order.

BlackLightPlus.Spacetime.geodesic_rhs — Method
geodesic_rhs(st::Spacetime, y::RayState) -> RayState

dy/dλ of the Hamiltonian flow. On a StationarySpacetime both halves come from one raise_and_norm at a position carrying three spatial dual partials: the value of the raised covector is dx^μ/dλ, the partials of the norm are ∂_i(2H), and dp_t/dλ is the exact zero. BlackLightPlus.Spacetime.geodesic_transport_rhs — Method
geodesic_transport_rhs(st::Spacetime, y::RayState, vs::NTuple{M,SVector{4}})
    -> (dy, dvs::NTuple{M,SVector{4}})

The geodesic right-hand side with the parallel transport dV^μ/dλ = -Γ^μ_{αβ} k^α V^β of M vectors along the ray (k^μ = dx^μ/dλ), from 2Γ^μ_{αβ} k^α V^β = g^{μλ}[(k·∂)g_{λβ} V^β + (V·∂)g_{λα} k^α - k^α V^β ∂_λ g_{αβ}]; no Christoffel symbol is formed. On a StationarySpacetime (∂_t g ≡ 0) one metric_and_inverse at the spatially seeded position gives g^{ab} with ∂_c g^{ab} (the geodesic, equal to geodesic_rhs up to round-off) and ∂_c g_{ab} (the transport). On a general spacetime the four columns of both gradients come from inverse_metric_gradient and metric_gradient, the time column included, so the transported frame follows a time-dependent metric. Runs in the number type of the state.

BlackLightPlus.Spacetime.helical_symmetry — Method
helical_symmetry(st, omega) -> Bool

Whether the map t → t + τ, (x, y) → R_z(ωτ)(x, y) (a rotation about z by ωτ, with the spatial components of every tensor rotated alike) carries st onto itself for every τ: the helical Killing vector ∂_t + ω ∂_φ. A stationary spacetime has it at ω = 0; Minkowski and KerrSchildCartesian (spin along z) at every ω; a BoostedSuperposedKerrSchild on a CircularOrbit at the orbit's frequency when every spin lies along z. Every other case answers false.

BlackLightPlus.Spacetime.image_crossings — Method
image_crossings(prep, θs, n::Integer) -> (m₁, m₂, k)

The polar turning-point counts of the k ∈ (0, 1, 2) crossings of the surface θs that form the image of order n of the screen point of prep (in the labelling of Krang, with every crossing in exactly one order). Let m₀ = 0 when the first leg of the ray, traced back from the observer, reaches the surface and m₀ = 1 otherwise. Ordinary rays and a surface at most as inclined as the observer's line of sight (|cos θs| ≤ |cos θo|, the equator among them): one crossing m = n + m₀. Ordinary rays and a surface closer to the pole: the pair {n, n + 1} when n ≡ m₀ (mod 2), none otherwise. Vortical rays: the pair {2n + m₀, 2n + m₀ + 1}. Crossings whose landing is not valid are kept; the caller tests valid.

BlackLightPlus.Spacetime.jacobi_amplitude — Method
jacobi_amplitude(u, m) -> φ

The Jacobi amplitude φ = am(u|m) for 0 ≤ m ≤ 1: A&S 16.13.4 for m < √ε; A&S 16.15.4, gd u + m₁ (sinh u cosh u − u) sech u / 4 with m₁ = 1 − m, only where m₁ cosh²u < √ε or m₁ = 0; the arithmetic–geometric mean (A&S 16.4) otherwise. The next term of the m → 1 expansion is of order (m₁ cosh²u)², so it fails as u approaches K(m) ≈ ln(4/√m₁), where m₁ cosh²u ≈ 4, whatever the size of m₁. Generic in the number type (ForwardDiff), with an immutable fixed-size buffer and no heap allocation (GPU).

BlackLightPlus.Spacetime.jacobi_sncndn — Method

The Jacobi elliptic functions sn(u|m), cn(u|m), dn(u|m) from jacobi_amplitude: sn = sin φ, cn = cos φ and dn = √(m₁ + m cos²φ), the form of √(1 − m sin²φ) free of cancellation as m → 1, for 0 ≤ m ≤ 1; the negative-parameter transformation (A&S 16.10) for m < 0 and the reciprocal-parameter transformation (A&S 16.11) for m > 1. Every sn, cn and sc of the closed-form radial inversion goes through this function.

BlackLightPlus.Spacetime.keplerian_specific_angular_momentum — Method
keplerian_specific_angular_momentum(a, r; prograde=true) -> l_K

Specific angular momentum l = -uphi/ut of a circular equatorial geodesic (Bardeen, Press & Teukolsky 1972, sign ±), M=1: lK = ±(r^2 ∓ 2 a sqrt(r) + a^2) / (r^{3/2} - 2 sqrt(r) ± a) — upper sign prograde, lower sign retrograde. At a=0 this is ±r^{3/2}/(r-2) (Schwarzschild). The prograde branch is > 0 with its minimum lms at the ISCO; the retrograde branch carries BPT's overall LEADING minus, so it is NEGATIVE (a counter-rotating orbit has uphi > 0, hence l = -uphi/u_t < 0).

BlackLightPlus.Spacetime.march_control — Method
march_control(st, y::RayState{T}, r_prev::T, b::MarchBudget{T}) -> MarchStep{T}

The fixed-control step at state y (r_prev the radius of the previous decision, Inf at the first):

  • KerrSchildCartesian: d = r - r_h on the Kerr-Schild radius r, stop = march_stop(d, E_n, r, r_prev, b), h = d / C (d w / C with the winding_factor w of a winding budget), R = r;
  • SuperposedKerrSchild and BoostedSuperposedKerrSchild: d = min_A(r_A - r_h,A) (-Inf on a non-finite r_A; the moving holes' r_A at the ray's time), the same rule on the coordinate radius R, h = d / C (min_A d_A w_A / C under winding), and a redshift cut of at most CAPTURE_REDSHIFT: the superposition's horizons are approximate, d stalls above zero while E_n grows, and a march continued beyond E_n ≈ 10² loses the ray (it comes out with garbage momenta); a state where g^{tt} ≥ 0 (a coarse step into a hole) captures through E_n = Inf;
  • ADMGrid: d is the lapse 1/√(-g^{tt}) minus alpha_min (lapse 1 and no redshift cut where g^{tt} ≥ 0), capture at d < 0, h = max(R, 1)/C on the coordinate radius R; a non-finite R captures;
  • any other spacetime: no capture surface, h = max(R, 1)/C, stop on escape or a non-finite R.

BlackLightPlus.Spacetime.winding_factor — Method
winding_factor(r, r_h, a) -> w

The factor w = (1 + q⁴)^(-1/4), q = 2|a|/(r - r_-) with r_- = a²/r_h, of the step distance of a march that resolves the frame-dragged winding near a spinning horizon. A ray within r - r_h of the outer horizon of a hole of spin a turns by dφ = 2a dr/Δ, Δ = (r - r_h)(r - r_-), in its azimuth about the spin axis (the Kerr–Schild azimuth of a backward-traced ray winds without bound as it nears the past horizon), so a step of radial extent (r - r_h)/C turns it by 2|a|/((r - r_-) C), up to 2|a|/((r_h - r_-) C) at the horizon, seven radians over C at a = 0.99. The step w (r - r_h)/C keeps the turn below about 1/C and leaves the step unchanged where r - r_- ≫ 2|a|; w = 1 at a = 0.
