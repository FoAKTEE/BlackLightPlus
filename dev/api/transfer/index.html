BlackLightPlus.Transfer — Module
Transfer

Radiative transfer along a ray: synchrotron coefficients of the thermal, power-law and kappa populations, fluid tetrads, segment operators and the unpolarized (StokesI) and polarized (IQUV) marches over a march control.

BlackLightPlus.Transfer.Kappa — Type
Kappa{T}
Kappa(frac, kappa, w)

Kappa electron population of index kappa and width w carrying the fraction frac of the electrons, with the constants of its synchrotron fits (Pandya et al. 2016; Marszewski et al. 2021): the low- and high-frequency emissivity and absorptivity normalisations jj_low, jj_high, aa_low, aa_high, aa_high_i and bridge exponents jj_x_i, aa_x_i; their Q and V factors jj_low_q, jj_low_v, jj_high_q, jj_high_v, aa_low_q, aa_low_v, aa_high_q, aa_high_v and exponents jj_x_q, jj_x_v, aa_x_q, aa_x_v; the Faraday-rotation factor rho_v = K₀(1/w)/K₂(1/w); and the rotativity fits bracketing kappa (rho_frac the blend weight between the bracketing indices, rho_q_low_a…e, rho_q_high_a…e, rho_v_low_a, rho_v_low_b, rho_v_high_a, rho_v_high_b), tabulated for kappa in [3.5, 5]. The constructor computes the constants once (blacklight PrecomputeKappaCoefficients) and is host only: the hypergeometric series is Float64. Kappa{T}(k) converts every field to T.

source
BlackLightPlus.Transfer.PowerLaw — Type
PowerLaw{T}
PowerLaw(frac, p, gamma_min, gamma_max)

Power-law electron population n(γ) ∝ γ^-p on gamma_min ≤ γ ≤ gamma_max carrying the fraction frac of the electrons, with the p-dependent constants of its synchrotron fits: jj, aa (emissivity and absorptivity normalisations, Marszewski et al. 2021), jj_q, jj_v, aa_q, aa_v (polarized emissivity and absorptivity factors), and rho, rho_q, rho_v (rotativity factors; rho_q is -Inf at the removable pole p = 2, where the fit takes its ln X limit). The constructor computes the constants once (blacklight PrecomputePowerLawCoefficients); PowerLaw{T}(pl) converts every field to T.

BlackLightPlus.Transfer.SpacetimeMarch — Type
SpacetimeMarch{S,T}(st, budget::MarchBudget{T})

Fixed-control march on the geodesic-lane spacetime st (scalars of type T, e.g. lane(KerrSchildCartesian(a), Float32)): at every step march_control(st, y, r_prev, budget) decides the stop and the affine step, and rk4_step advances the state. The step limit is budget.nmax.

BlackLightPlus.Transfer.StoredPath — Type
StoredPath{S,V,D}(st, ys, dls)

March along a stored trajectory of the spacetime st: the states ys ordered source to camera and dls[n] the affine step from state n to n + 1 (as Decks.sampled_trace and the trace products return them). The march visits the states camera → source, pairs the sample at state n with the step dls[n-1] behind it, and transports the polarized camera frame over each stored step with RK4. The step limit is length(ys). isbits after Adapt.adapt to a device.

BlackLightPlus.Transfer.TransferParams — Type
TransferParams{T,P,K,E,C,N}
TransferParams(units, electrons, cuts, nu_obs; powerlaw = nothing, kappa = nothing)

The parameter set a march reads at every sample: the code-unit to cgs conversions units::Units{T} (its length unit L_unit turns an affine step into a cgs path length, ds = h L_unit / ν_obs for k_t = -1 at the camera), the electrons::ElectronModel (its thermal_frac is the thermal population fraction), the plasma cuts::PlasmaCuts, the optional nonthermal populations powerlaw::Union{Nothing,PowerLaw} and kappa::Union{Nothing,Kappa} (nothing removes a population at compile time) and the observing frequency nu_obs (Hz). The camera polarization vertical is the program's CameraFrame.up, not a field here.

isbits whenever its fields are. Each sub-record keeps its own number type, so a derivative enters only where it is seeded: Units(m_bh, Dual(mass_scale)) gives T = Dual with the electron model, the populations and nu_obs left Float64; a Dual rat_high makes E = ElectronModel{Dual}. A mass-scale ladder is an NTuple{K} of sets differing only in units.

BlackLightPlus.Spacetime.march_control — Method
march_control(ctl, y, r_prev, it) -> MarchStep

The decision at step it of a march in state y (r_prev the R of the previous decision): for a SpacetimeMarch the spacetime's fixed-control rule march_control(ctl.st, y, r_prev, ctl.budget); for a StoredPath a stop once the stored states are exhausted, the stored step behind the current state (zero at the source end) and R = r_prev.

coefficients(::IQUV, p::TransferParams, state::PlasmaState, kcov, kcon, g, ν_obs,

BlackLightPlus.Transfer.coefficients — Method
coefficients(::StokesI, p::TransferParams, state::PlasmaState, kcov, ν_obs) -> (j, α)

Invariant Stokes-I emissivity j = j_ν/ν² and absorptivity α = α_ν ν (cgs) of the decoded sample state for the photon wave covector kcov at observing frequency ν_obs. The pitch angle comes from the invariant identity cos²θ_B = (k·b)² / ((k·u)² b²) (b·u = 0, k null), clipped at 1, with no tetrad; the fluid-frame frequency is -k·u ν_obs. The thermal fit (p.electrons.thermal_frac) is followed by the power-law and kappa fits when present. An invalid state returns zeros of the promoted unit and electron-model type, which segment treats as a no-op. The kinematics are read through kinematic in the type of kcov.

BlackLightPlus.Transfer.fluid_tetrad — Method
fluid_tetrad(ucon, ucov, kcon, kcov, up_con, gcov, gcon; renorm_e1 = false) -> SMatrix{4,4}

Orthonormal tetrad of the observer u (both index positions) with e_(3) along the photon wave vector k and e_(2) along the spacelike direction up_con (the magnetic field b^μ for the fluid frame, the image vertical for the camera frame); row a holds e_(a)^μ. renorm_e1 = true renormalises e_(1) with gcov, required wherever |det g| ≠ 1. See fluid_tetrad_checked.

BlackLightPlus.Transfer.fluid_tetrad_checked — Method
fluid_tetrad_checked(ucon, ucov, kcon, kcov, up_con, gcov, gcon; renorm_e1 = false)
    -> (tet::SMatrix{4,4}, ok::Bool)

fluid_tetrad with a validity flag. When up_con lies in span(u, k) to working precision (squared norm of its projection at most 1e-12 of up·up), the coordinate axis least aligned with e_(3) is projected instead (ipole set_Econ_from_trial); the pitch angle is then zero and the frame choice does not affect the coefficients. ok is false when no spacelike e_(2) exists (u not unit timelike or k not null to working precision); e_(2) is then zero, never NaN.

BlackLightPlus.Transfer.kinematic — Method
kinematic(::Type{V}, x)

x (a scalar or an SVector) in the number type V of the photon wave vector and the metric. The kinematic fields of a PlasmaState (ucon, ucov, bcon, bcov, b_sq) are functions of the primitives and the metric only; when the state's number type is promoted by a derivative that the parameters alone carry (a Dual mass scale or electron temperature ratio), their partials are zero by construction and dropping them is exact. A derivative carried by the primitives or the metric must therefore be carried by V too. The identity when x already has type V.

BlackLightPlus.Transfer.march — Method
march(::IQUV, y0::RayState{Tg}, prog) -> SVector{4,Float64}

Observed (I, Q, U, V) (cgs, ν_obs³ times the invariant Stokes vector) of the camera ray y0 in the camera polarization frame: the normal observer at the camera with e_(2) along prog.frame.up, parallel-transported with the geodesic by RK4 in the lane Tg (geodesic_transport_rhs). Each valid sample contributes its Mueller operator in the fluid tetrad, rotated into the transported frame, and the accumulated Stokes vector is kept admissible. The march also ends where the transported frame admits no spacelike screen leg (fluid_tetrad_checked), i.e. the photon momentum has drifted off the null cone on a step far too coarse for the local curvature. params is one Float64 TransferParams. Pure and allocation-free.

source
BlackLightPlus.Transfer.march — Method
march(::StokesI, y0::RayState{Tg}, prog) -> output_type(StokesI(), prog.params)

Observed Stokes I of the camera ray y0 = [x^μ; p_μ] (p = -k, p_t = 1): the ray is advanced under prog.control in the lane Tg of y0 for at most max_steps(prog.control) steps; each sample of prog.provider that emits or absorbs contributes its operator from segment, and the result is I_ν = ν_obs³ Σ_n B_n Π_{k<n} A_k (cgs). With an NTuple{K} of parameter sets (a mass-scale ladder; grid providers only) the primitives are sampled once per step and the result is an SVector{K,Float64} whose lane k performs the operations of the march of set k alone. Pure and allocation-free.

BlackLightPlus.Transfer.segment — Method
segment(::IQUV, j, α, ρ, ds; dtau_max = POL_DELTA_TAU_MAX) -> (M::SMatrix{4,4}, b::SVector{4})

Affine operator S → M S + b of one constant-coefficient segment of invariant length ds with invariant coefficients j, α, ρ (SVector{4}, U components zero), by cases: emission only, total absorption (αP = ρP = 0), pure rotation (αI = 0), and the full absorption and rotation solution (Landi Degl'Innocenti 1985). A segment with `δτ = αI dsabovedtaumaxis saturated:M = 0,b = K⁻¹ j. Inputs outside the Stokes cone (|jP| > jIorαP > αI, which the fits give at small pitch angles) are rescaled onto it, keepingjI,αIand the polarization direction, so the segment decays and emits an admissible Stokes vector.ρP = 0takes the full solution; a degenerate eigenstructure (λ₁² + λ₂²orα_I² - λ₁²below1e-12` of its scale) takes total-absorption coupling.

BlackLightPlus.Transfer.segment — Method
segment(::StokesI, j, α, ds; dtau_max = POL_DELTA_TAU_MAX) -> (A, B)

Affine operator I → A I + B of one segment of invariant length ds with invariant emissivity j and absorptivity α (blacklight UnpolarizedTransferStep): A = e^{-δτ}, B = (j/α) e^{-δτ} expm1(δτ) with δτ = α ds; saturated (A = 0, B = j/α) above dtau_max; emission only (A = 1, B = j ds) for α ≤ 0. Generic in the number type.

BlackLightPlus.Transfer.thermal_synchrotron — Method
thermal_synchrotron(thermal_frac, n_e, theta_e, B, nu, sin_th) -> (j, α)

Unpolarized thermal synchrotron emissivity and absorptivity in invariant form (M 28, 30, 31) for electron density n_e (cm⁻³), temperature theta_e, field B (G), fluid-frame frequency nu (Hz) and pitch angle sin_th; α from Kirchhoff's law, zero where 1/α² is not finite. source
BlackLightPlus.Transfer.thermal_synchrotron_polarized — Method
thermal_synchrotron_polarized(thermal_frac, n_e, theta_e, B, nu, sin_th, cos_th) -> NTuple{8}

Thermal synchrotron polarized coefficients (j_I, j_Q, j_V, α_I, α_Q, α_V, ρ_Q, ρ_V) (M 28, 30, 31, 33–37); cos_th is signed along the propagation and sets the sign of the circular terms. The absorptivities follow from Kirchhoff's law and are zero where 1/α_I² is not finite. ρ_V takes its cold-plasma factor 1 below K_THETA_E_ZERO; the ρ_Q conversion factor is weighted by a C¹ smoothstep over [K_THETA_E_BLEND_LO, K_THETA_E_ZERO] and is zero below it. Eight Float64 zeros for any non-positive input or a zero thermal_frac. After blacklight ThermalSynchrotronPolarized.
