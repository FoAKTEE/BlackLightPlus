BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.Bias — Type
Bias{T}(b0)
Bias(sphere::UniformSphere)

Importance bias of the exponential-transform scattering decision, b = max(1, b0 (n_sc + 1)²) after n_sc scatterings (bias): a constant b0 > 1 raises the rate of rare scatterings and the growth with order keeps the higher orders populated. The sphere form is b0 = n_e σ_T R, its Thomson depth. The estimator is unbiased for any b ≥ 1.

BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.CurvedMarch — Type
CurvedMarch{S,T}(st, r_h, C, escape, R_em, nmax)
CurvedMarch(st; C = 80.0, escape = 1.0e3, R_em = 100.0, nmax = CURVED_MAX_STEPS)

Geodesic march for Monte Carlo transport on the spacetime st, whose horizon proxy r_h is horizon_floor(st) in the keyword form: with (d, r) = proxy_distance(st, x) (the distance above the horizon proxy and the escape radius: Kerr–Schild radius and r - r_h on Kerr, the coordinate radius and the nearest hole's r_A - r_h,A on a binary, at the ray's time when the metric moves) the affine step is h = d/(C (|k| + 10⁻³⁰⁰)), |k| the spatial norm of k^μ = g^{μν} p_ν, so the coordinate advance is about d/C whatever the photon energy (grmonty stepsize); the state advances by rk4_step. The photon is captured at d < HORIZON_EPS_SAMPLED (or a non-finite d) and escapes at r > escape, or at r > R_em while outbound: beyond the emission-confinement radius R_em it meets no more plasma. At most nmax steps; the R of its decisions is r.

BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.FlatMarch — Type
FlatMarch{T}(R, step_frac, nmax)
FlatMarch(sphere::UniformSphere; step_frac = SPHERE_STEP_FRAC, nmax = SPHERE_MAX_STEPS)

Straight-line march in flat coordinates for a ball of radius R: the wave vector k^μ = η^{μν} p_ν is constant, a step advances x += k h with h = step_frac R/|k| (h = R for a vanishing spatial k), and the photon escapes after a step that leaves it at |x| > R. The emission point itself is not tested. Without escape the march ends unrecorded after nmax steps. Answers the Transfer control protocol (march_control, geodesic_step, max_steps); the R of its decisions is the squared radius |x|².

BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.FluidFrame — Type
FluidFrame()
StaticFrame()

How the fluid-frame frequency and the pitch angle of an absorption rate are formed. FluidFrame: from the state's four-vectors, ν = −(p·u) mₑc²/h and cos θ = (p·b)/(√b² (−p·u)) (grmonty get_bk_angle). StaticFrame: for a fluid at rest with its field along x¹ in flat coordinates, from the contravariant wave vector, ν = k^t mₑc²/h and cos θ = k¹/|k|. The two agree in exact arithmetic on a null k and differ in rounding. rate_frame picks the frame of a provider.

BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.generate — Method
generate(zone::EmissionZone, tables::GenerationTables, rng::MCRng) -> (Superphoton, MCRng)

One superphoton emitted by zone and the advanced stream. The frequency is drawn by rejection against tables (weight w(ν) of the packet), the pitch angle to the field by rejection against jmax, the azimuth uniformly; the momentum is null in the emission tetrad of the zone and covariant in coordinates, the weight is w(ν), the optical depths and scattering count are zero, and the emission diagnostics are the zone's x¹, x², n_e, Θₑ, B. Each rejection loop stops after REJECT_MAX_ITER proposals with the last one. Pure and allocation-free.

BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.int_jnu_zone — Method
int_jnu_zone(n_e, theta_e, B, nu) -> Float64

Angle-integrated emissivity ∫ j_ν dΩ (erg s⁻¹ cm⁻³ Hz⁻¹), the per-volume emission rate of a zone at frequency nu. j_ν depends on direction through sin θ only and is symmetric about the equator, so ∫ j_ν dΩ = 4π ∫₀^{π/2} j_ν(sin θ) sin θ dθ, evaluated by the composite Simpson rule on N_ZONE_THETA intervals (fixed trip count, no allocation).

BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.tabulated_dnmax — Method
tabulated_dnmax(gt::IntJnuTable, wgt::WeightTable) -> Float64

Rejection envelope of tabulated generation: the maximum over the nodes of gt of int_jnu_at / (weight_at + WEIGHT_FLOOR). Both logarithms are piecewise linear in ln ν between these nodes when every weight node is a node of gt, so the maximum over the nodes bounds the ratio at every frequency and the acceptance is at most 1.

BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.tally! — Method
tally!(sink::Sink, sph::Superphoton, loss, spawned) -> nothing

Receive the scattering ledger of the photon sph (as it entered the tracker): loss = Σ w (1 − e^{−dτ_s}), the incident weight lost to scattering along the march, and spawned, the total weight of the children it spawned. BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.track! — Method
track!(ctl, st, provider, opacities::Opacities, sph::Superphoton, rng::MCRng, sink;
       absorb = Val(true), scatter = Val(false)) -> (sph, recorded, rng)

March the superphoton sph under the control ctl (FlatMarch, CurvedMarch) on the spacetime st through the plasma of provider (Matter.sample with opacities.params) until the control stops it. Every step accrues the trapezoid absorption depth (absorb = Val(true), rate absorption_rate in the provider's rate_frame) and scattering depth (scatter = Val(true), scattering_rate with opacities.table), and decays the weight by e^{−(dτ_a + dτ_s)}. With scattering, a step with dτ_s > 0 that may_scatter while nscatt < opacities.max_scatt draws one scatter_event with bias bias(opacities.bias, nscatt); a spawn advances to the event point, kicks the momentum there off the plasma of scatter_site by scatter_superphoton with this photon's stream, and hands the child (weight additionally absorbed up to the event, nscatt + 1, the depths at the step start) to spawn!(sink, child, rng, event), event counting the spawn decisions from 0. The returned photon has the final state and depths; if the control reports an escape it is also handed to record!(sink, sph), and with scattering the ledger goes to tally!. rng is the advanced stream; without scattering it is not drawn from. Allocation-free for isbits arguments and sink.
