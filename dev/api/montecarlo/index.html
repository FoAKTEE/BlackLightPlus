BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.Bias — Type
Bias{T}(b0)
Bias(sphere::UniformSphere)

Constant importance bias of the scattering events, b = max(1, b0 (n_sc + 1)²) after n_sc scatterings (bias): a constant b0 > 1 raises the rate of rare scatterings and the growth with order keeps the higher orders populated. BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.CurvedMarch — Type
CurvedMarch{S,T}(st, r_h, C, escape, R_em, nmax, r_plunge)
CurvedMarch(st; C = 80.0, escape = 1.0e3, R_em = 100.0, nmax = CURVED_MAX_STEPS,
            plunge = true)

Geodesic march for Monte Carlo transport on the spacetime st, whose horizon proxy r_h is horizon_floor(st) in the keyword form: with (d, r) = proxy_distance(st, x) (the distance above the horizon proxy and the escape radius: Kerr–Schild radius and r - r_h on Kerr, the coordinate radius and the nearest hole's r_A - r_h,A on a binary, at the ray's time when the metric moves) the affine step is h = d/(C (|k| + 10⁻³⁰⁰)), |k| the spatial norm of k^μ = g^{μν} p_ν, so the coordinate advance is about d/C whatever the photon energy (grmonty stepsize); the state advances by rk4_step. The photon is captured at d < HORIZON_EPS_SAMPLED (or a non-finite d) and escapes at r > escape, or at r > R_em while outbound: beyond the emission-confinement radius R_em it meets no more plasma. At most nmax steps; the R of its decisions is r. A photon that may spawn no more children is captured once a step moves it inward below r_plunge (plunging): plunge_radius(st) with plunge = true, -Inf (never) otherwise.

BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.EmissivityTable — Type
EmissivityTable{V}(l0, l1, l2, h1, h2, n1, n2, lnF, slope)

The angle integral F(K) = ∫₀^{π/2} sin²θ G(K/sin θ) dθ of the thermal synchrotron fit, G(X) = (X^{1/2} + 2^{11/12} X^{1/6})² e^{−X^{1/3}}, through which ∫ j_ν dΩ = 4π n_e e² ν_c/c · (√2 π/27) · F(K), K = ν/((2/9) ν_c Θₑ²), ν_c = eB/(2π mₑ c) (int_jnu). Nodes in l = ln K: n1 intervals of width h1 from l0 to l1, then n2 of width h2 from l1 to l2; lnF[k] and slope[k] = d ln F/d ln K at node k, both from the Simpson rule on F_TABLE_THETA intervals. synchrotron_F interpolates ln F by cubic Hermite polynomials. isbits after Adapt.adapt to a device. The process-wide table is EMISSIVITY_TABLE.

BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.FlatMarch — Type
FlatMarch{T}(R, step_frac, nmax)
FlatMarch(sphere::UniformSphere; step_frac = SPHERE_STEP_FRAC, nmax = SPHERE_MAX_STEPS)

Straight-line march in flat coordinates for a ball of radius R: the wave vector k^μ = η^{μν} p_ν is constant, a step advances x += k h with h = step_frac R/|k| (h = R for a vanishing spatial k), and the photon escapes after a step that leaves it at |x| > R. The emission point itself is not tested. Without escape the march ends unrecorded after nmax steps. Answers the Transfer control protocol (march_control, geodesic_step, max_steps); the R of its decisions is the squared radius |x|².

BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.FluidFrame — Type
FluidFrame()
StaticFrame()

How the fluid-frame frequency and the pitch angle of an absorption rate are formed. FluidFrame: from the state's four-vectors, ν = −(p·u) mₑc²/h and cos θ = (p·b)/(√b² (−p·u)) (grmonty get_bk_angle). StaticFrame: for a fluid at rest with its field along x¹ in flat coordinates, from the contravariant wave vector, ν = k^t mₑc²/h and cos θ = k¹/|k|. The two agree in exact arithmetic on a null k and differ in rounding. rate_frame picks the frame of a provider.

BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.MarchLedger — Type
MarchLedger

The weight and power account of one tracked photon, handed to tally!. With Δτ = Δτ_a + Δτ_s of a step and w the weight at its start, the step removes w (1 − e^{−Δτ}): w Δτ_a (1 − e^{−Δτ})/Δτ absorbed and w Δτ_s (1 − e^{−Δτ})/Δτ scattered, the expected weight of the step's children. Fields: outcome (ESCAPED_MARCH, CAPTURED_MARCH, DROPPED_MARCH); scatter_loss, the scattered weight of the steps that may spawn; scatter_loss_final, that of the steps that may not (the order max_scatt); spawned, the weight of the children spawned; unspawned, the weight of the events that spawned nothing (an event site outside the plasma, a child weight at most weight_min, an ordinal beyond the stream layout); absorbed, Σ w E Δτ_a (1 − e^{−Δτ})/Δτ (mₑc², E = −p_t at the step start); w and E, the final weight and energy; events, the number of event ordinals used. Per photon, w_start = w + Σ (absorbed + scattered weights) up to rounding, and the expectation of spawned + unspawned is scatter_loss.

BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.StateBias — Type
StateBias(b0, w_ref, b_max, split = 1, order = 1)

Importance bias of the scattering events that follows the plasma and the photon: at a step of a photon of weight w, scattered n times, through gas of temperature Θₑ, b = clamp(b0 A(Θₑ) (w/w_ref) order^n, 1, b_max) (amplification). With order = 1 a child, of weight about w/b, carries about w_ref/(b0 A) photons and the energy weight w_ref E/b0 whatever the gas that scattered it, and its own bias is near 1, so the light scattered two and three times is sampled little; order > 1 raises the bias of every later generation by that factor per scattering, which samples the higher orders and the high-frequency tail they make. b_max bounds the events per step. With split > 1, an event whose uncapped bias exceeds b_max spawns m = min(split, ⌈b_uncapped/b_max⌉) children of 1/m of the event weight, each with its own kick. Any positive b chosen from the state before the step's draws, and any split, leave the estimator unbiased.

BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.amplification — Method
amplification(theta_e) -> Float64

A(Θₑ) = 1 + 4Θₑ + 16Θₑ², the closed form that joins the two limits of the mean energy gain of one Thomson scattering off a Maxwell–Jüttner population, 1 + 4Θₑ K₃(1/Θₑ)/K₂(1/Θₑ) (1 + 4Θₑ cold, 16Θₑ² hot): at most 15 % above it for every Θₑ (157 against 146.85 at Θₑ = 3). Any positive function of the state serves StateBias; this one costs no Bessel function.

BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.emission_tetrad — Method
emission_tetrad(ucon, ucov, bcon, gcov, gcon) -> SMatrix{4,4}

The orthonormal emission tetrad, row a holding e_(a)^μ: e_(0) = u, e_(1) along the field b projected orthogonal to u (the pitch-angle axis), and e_(2), e_(3) by Gram–Schmidt on the x² and x³ directions under the metric gcov. A field of metric norm at most SMALL_VECTOR is replaced by the x¹ direction. Where the part of x² (x³) orthogonal to the vectors before it has less than TETRAD_MIN_RESIDUAL of the axis's squared norm, as for a field along x³, the coordinate axis with the largest such part takes its place, so the frame stays orthonormal to rounding for any field. ucov and gcon are not read. Not the imaging tetrad, whose e_(3) is along the photon.

BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.generate — Method
generate(zone::EmissionZone, tables::GenerationTables, rng::MCRng) -> (Superphoton, MCRng)

One superphoton emitted by zone and the advanced stream. The frequency is drawn by rejection against tables (log-uniform proposals accepted with (∫j_ν dΩ/w(ν))/dnmax; weight w(ν) of the packet), the pitch angle to the field by rejection against jmax, the azimuth uniformly; the momentum is null in the emission tetrad of the zone and covariant in coordinates, the weight is w(ν), the optical depths and scattering count are zero, and the emission diagnostics are the zone's x¹, x², n_e, Θₑ, B. Each rejection loop stops after REJECT_MAX_ITER proposals with the last one. Pure and allocation-free. Compiled as a function of its own, never inlined, so every program that generates from a zone runs the same machine code, bit for bit, whatever surrounds the call (its dot products are @simd reductions, whose rounding follows the inlining context).

BlackLightPlus.MonteCarlo.track! — Method
track!(ctl, st, provider, opacities::Opacities, sph::Superphoton, rng::MCRng, sink;
       absorb = Val(true), scatter = Val(false)) -> (sph, recorded, rng)

March the superphoton sph under the control ctl (FlatMarch, CurvedMarch) on the spacetime st through the plasma of provider (Matter.sample with opacities.params) until the control stops it. Every step accrues the trapezoid absorption depth (absorb = Val(true), rate absorption_rate in the provider's rate_frame) and scattering depth (scatter = Val(true), scattering_rate with opacities.table), and decays the weight by e^{−(dτ_a + dτ_s)}. With scattering, while nscatt < opacities.max_scatt every step with dτ_s > 0 draws its Poisson-many events (next_event) under the bias event_bias of opacities.bias (the step's larger end temperature, the weight at the step start), each spawning children of weight event_weight (split in copies) at its event point, kicked by scatter_superphoton off the plasma of scatter_site on the child_stream of its ordinal, with nscatt + 1 and the depths at the step start, handed to spawn!; an event whose site is not valid spawns nothing. A photon that may spawn no more stops captured when it plunges (plunging). The returned photon has the final state and depths; if the control reports an escape it is also handed to record!(sink, sph), and its MarchLedger goes to tally!. rng is the advanced stream; without scattering it is not drawn from. Allocation-free for isbits arguments and sink.
