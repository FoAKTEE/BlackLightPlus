BlackLightPlus.Plans — Module
Plans

Run configuration, precision lanes and prepare, which turns a configuration, a spacetime and a matter source into a backend-free Plan.

BlackLightPlus.Plans.CameraConfig — Type
CameraConfig(; inclination_deg = 60.0, distance = 1000.0, fov = 40.0, resolution = 64)

Camera geometry: observer inclination inclination_deg (degrees from the spin axis), camera distance distance and full field of view fov (both in M = GM/c²), and resolution pixels per side.

BlackLightPlus.Plans.Lanes — Type
Lanes{Tg,Tt,A}()
Lanes()

Uniform precision lanes: every ray's geodesic (and, polarized, its transported camera frame) is marched in Tg, the transfer in Tt and the image accumulated in A. Lanes() is Lanes{Float64,Float64,Float64}(); Lanes{Float32,Float64,Float64}() marches every geodesic in Float32, which fails near the critical curve (see MixedLanes). The marches run the transfer and the accumulator in Float64, so prepare accepts Tt = A = Float64 only.

source
BlackLightPlus.Plans.MixedLanes — Type
MixedLanes(rescue_px = 2)

Mixed precision lanes on Kerr: the geodesics of the pixels within rescue_px pixels (Chebyshev distance) of the critical curve, where a Float32 march loses the ray, are marched in Float64 and queued first; every other pixel is marched in Float32. The transfer and the accumulator are Float64.

BlackLightPlus.Plans.Plan — Type
Plan

The prepared run that every product consumes; built by prepare, never by hand. Fields:

  • config::RunConfig, the configuration it was prepared from;
  • spacetime, the Float64 spacetime of the transfer lane (the plasma decode, the wave vector, the tetrads and the camera rays);
  • geodesic_spacetime, the same spacetime on the geodesic lane (lane(spacetime, Tg); the Float32 bulk lane under MixedLanes);
  • provider, the matter (a PlasmaProvider, or nothing for products without plasma);
  • mode, the transfer mode (StokesI() or IQUV());
  • params::TransferParams, units, electron model, cuts, nonthermal populations and frequency (nothing for the Monte Carlo sphere, whose plasma is cgs and whose coordinates are centimetres);
  • camera::PlaneParallelCamera, frame::CameraFrame{Float64} and pixels, the PixelGrid of the camera's pixel centres;
  • budget::MarchBudget{Float64} (C, escape, R_em, max_steps) and geodesic_budget, the same budget on the geodesic lane;
  • horizon, the spacetime's horizon proxy (horizon_radius(spacetime): the Kerr outer horizon, the per-hole radii of a binary, the lapse floor of an ADM grid; NaN when the spacetime has none);
  • escape = escape_factor × distance and R_em = domain_radius(provider) (Inf without matter), in M;
  • theta_o, the observer inclination in radians;
  • curve, the Kerr critical curve (α, β) at the scheduling inclination (curve_inclination), empty vectors on other spacetimes;
  • lanes, the precision lanes; queue, the pixel queue order (a permutation of 1:npixels: the queue given to prepare, else longest predicted march first on Kerr and the identity elsewhere); rescue, the ascending pixels marched on the Float64 lane under MixedLanes (empty otherwise);
  • hotcross, the memo of hotcross_table: empty until a Monte Carlo scattering run of the plan first needs the table.

BlackLightPlus.Plans.RunConfig — Type
RunConfig(; m_bh_cgs = 6.2e9 × 1.989e33, mass_scale_cgs = 1e24, frequency_hz = 230e9,
          camera = CameraConfig(), plasma = PlasmaConfig(), max_steps = 2500,
          C = 80.0, escape_factor = 1.1)

One run: the hole mass m_bh_cgs (g), the density mass scale mass_scale_cgs (g), the observing frequency frequency_hz (Hz), the camera and the plasma model, and the march budget: at most max_steps steps of affine length (distance to the horizon proxy)/C, a ray escaping beyond escape_factor × camera.distance. The spacetime, the matter, the transfer mode and the precision lanes are arguments of prepare.

BlackLightPlus.Plans.prepare — Method
prepare(cfg::RunConfig; spacetime, matter, mode = IQUV(), lanes = Lanes(), tile = 16,
        queue = nothing) -> Plan

The plan of a run of cfg on spacetime (a Float64 spacetime value: Kerr, a binary, an ADM grid, a user metric) through matter (a plasma provider: a snapshot, a uniform grid, a frame window, an analytic source; or nothing for products that need no plasma). mode is the transfer mode of the imaging products, lanes the precision lanes (Lanes, MixedLanes), tile the side of the image tiles of the Kerr queue order. queue, a permutation of the pixel indices 1:resolution², is the order in which render, sensitivity, variants and shadow queue the pixels in place of the derived one: for the next frame of a movie, for example, march_sort_order(vec(shadow(plan).steps)) queues the longest marches of the previous frame first. The queue order changes no output bit.

Derived here, once: the camera and its frame, the pixel centres, the transfer parameters (transfer_params), the escape radius escape_factor × distance, the emission radius R_em = domain_radius(matter) beyond which the march stops on an outbound ray, the march budget on both lanes, the horizon proxy, the observer inclination, the Kerr critical curve with the cost-ordered queue (identity order on other spacetimes) unless queue is given and, for MixedLanes, the rescue set. Throws ArgumentError for a spacetime that is not Float64, a geodesic lane the spacetime does not have, transfer or accumulator lanes other than Float64, MixedLanes off Kerr, or a queue that is not a permutation of the pixel indices (queue_items).

BlackLightPlus.Plans.prepare — Method
prepare(sphere::UniformSphere) -> Plan

The plan of the Monte Carlo sphere: the flat spacetime Minkowski() with sphere as its matter, in centimetres. The coordinates are cm and the parameter set is nothing: the sphere's plasma is given in cgs and reads no units, electron model or cuts, and the Monte Carlo opacities of a plan without a parameter set take one centimetre per coordinate length. The configuration is RunConfig(); its camera, march budget and queue are not read by the Monte Carlo, and a plan without a parameter set is not imaged.

source
BlackLightPlus.Plans.transfer_params — Method
transfer_params(cfg::RunConfig; mass_scale = cfg.mass_scale_cgs,
                rat_high = cfg.plasma.rat_high) -> TransferParams

The march parameter set of cfg: Units(m_bh_cgs, mass_scale), the electron model of cfg.plasma, the cuts sigma ≤ sigma_cut and theta_e ≥ theta_e_min (a negative value turns a cut off), the power-law population when power_frac != 0, the kappa population when kappa_frac != 0, and the observing frequency frequency_hz. mass_scale or rat_high may be a ForwardDiff.Dual (the units or the electron model then carry the derivative), or another value of the mass scale (one set of a mass-scale ladder).

BlackLightPlus.Plans.hotcross_table — Method
hotcross_table(plan::Plan) -> HotcrossTable

The hot-cross-section table of the plan's Monte Carlo scattering, build_hotcross_table(), built at the first request and kept in the plan, so later runs of the plan reuse the same table (and its device copies). Concurrent first requests may each build it; the tables are identical.
