ElectronModel{T}

Electron thermodynamics of the simulation model: mean molecular weight mu (mₚ), electrons per ion ne_ni, whether the fluid pressure is the total pressure (use_p) or the internal energy fixes the temperature, the rat_low/rat_high ion-to-electron temperature ratios at β → 0 and β → ∞, the fluid, ion and electron adiabatic indices gamma, gamma_i, gamma_e, and thermal_frac, the thermal population fraction. One emitting fluid element, the input of superphoton generation, in signature (−,+,+,+) with time first.

Fields: electron density n_e (cm⁻³), temperature theta_e = k Tₑ/mₑc², field strength B (G), proper emitting volume dV = √−g d³x L³ (cm³), the single factor that turns the angle-integrated emissivity into a rate, the emission point x, the fluid four-velocity ucon/ucov, the field four-vector bcon (its spatial direction fixes the field axis), and the metric gcov and inverse gcon at x.

BlackLightPlus.Matter.PageThorneDisk — Type
PageThorneDisk{T} <: AnalyticProvider
PageThorneDisk(a; mdot = 1.0, r_out = 50.0, n_e_max = 1e6, theta_e_max = 1.0,
               beta = 10.0, scale_height = 0.05, prograde = true)

Page–Thorne thin accretion disk around a Kerr hole of spin a (M = 1), as a volumetric source: a Gaussian layer of relative half-thickness scale_height (H/r) about the equator between the ISCO and r_out, with density n_e_max F/F_peak (cm⁻³) and temperature theta_e_max (F/F_peak)^¼ in the plane, |B| from the plasma beta, on Keplerian circular orbits. mdot scales the flux.

A negative spin is the disk of spin |a| with the rotation sense flipped: the record stores a = |a| and the flipped prograde. The flux closed form is written for a co-rotating disk; a counter-rotating disk uses it at the effective spin a_flux = −a (Bardeen, Press & Teukolsky 1972, lower sign).

Fields: a, a_flux, mdot, r_isco, r_out, the roots x0 (= √rISCO), x1, x2, x3, `fpeak, the largest flux on 4001 uniform nodes of[rISCO, rout](soF/Fpeakmay exceed 1 between nodes),nemax,thetaemax,beta,scaleheight,prograde`.

The analytic state ignores the units, electron model and cuts of the parameter set.

References

Page & Thorne (1974); Bardeen, Press & Teukolsky (1972).

BlackLightPlus.Matter.PlasmaProvider — Type
PlasmaProvider

A source of plasma for the transfer coefficients and the Monte Carlo tracker. Every provider implements domain_radius(p), the coordinate radius beyond which it is vacuum, and is sampled by sample(p, st, x, params). PlasmaState{T}

BlackLightPlus.Matter.PolishDoughnut — Type
PolishDoughnut{T} <: AnalyticProvider
PolishDoughnut(a; l0 = nothing, lambda = nothing, r_center = nothing,
               n_e_center = 1e7, theta_e_center = 50.0, polytrope_n = 1.5,
               beta = 10.0, W_surface = nothing, fill_factor = 0.9, prograde = true)

Constant-ℓ equilibrium torus around a Kerr hole of spin a (M = 1).

The specific angular momentum comes from the first given of r_center (ℓ = ℓ_K(r_center)), l0, or lambda ∈ (0, 1) (ℓ = λ (ℓ_mb − ℓ_ms) + ℓ_ms), default lambda = 0.5; a torus exists only for |ℓ| beyond the marginally stable |ℓ_ms| toward ℓ_mb, otherwise ArgumentError. n_e_center (cm⁻³) and theta_e_center are the central density and temperature, polytrope_n the polytropic index, beta the plasma beta fixing |B|. The surface is W_surface if given, else the cusp potential (Roche-lobe filling) when the cusp is bound, else fill_factor times the central potential so that the outer edge stays finite. prograde selects the rotation sense.

Fields: a, l0, polytrope_n, beta, n_e_center, theta_e_center, the Boyer–Lindquist radii r_cusp, r_center, r_out of the cusp, the centre and the outer equatorial edge, the potentials W_surface and W_center, and prograde.

The analytic state ignores the units, electron model and cuts of the parameter set.

References

Abramowicz, Jaroszyński & Sikora (1978); Komissarov (2006).

BlackLightPlus.Matter.UniformSphere — Type
UniformSphere{T} <: AnalyticProvider
UniformSphere(; n_e = 1e13, theta_e = 100.0, B = 1.0, R = 1.0)

A ball of radius R of static, uniform, thermal synchrotron plasma in flat spacetime: density n_e (cm⁻³), temperature theta_e, field strength B (G), and the emitting volume dV = (4/3)π R³. Lengths are in the units of the spacetime coordinates (cm for the Monte Carlo sphere).

The fluid is at rest, u^μ = (1, 0, 0, 0), and the unit field direction is b^μ = (0, 1, 0, 0) along x¹, which keeps the field off the x³ axis of the emission tetrad's Gram–Schmidt order. These are the kinematics of Minkowski(); covariant components follow from the metric the caller supplies.

The analytic state ignores the units, electron model and cuts of the parameter set.

The plasma state of the primitives prim at a point whose metric pair is g = (gcov, gcon). BlackLightPlus.Matter.domain_radius — Method
domain_radius(src::EquatorialHotspot) -> Float64

Inf when ambient plasma fills the domain. Otherwise the smallest integer above √(r_orbit² + a²) + 4 R_spot: the Kerr-Schild spatial metric δ_ij + f l_i l_j (f ≥ 0) dominates the Euclidean one and (u·dx)² ≥ 0, so the cut ds < 4 R_spot confines the spot to a Euclidean distance below 4 R_spot from a centre on the circle of radius √(r_orbit² + a²), and the Kerr-Schild radius never exceeds the Euclidean one.

BlackLightPlus.Matter.gfactor_circular — Method
gfactor_circular(rs, a, b, λ, η; sub_kep = 1, betar = 1, betaphi = 1) -> g

Redshift factor g = ν_obs/ν_emit of a photon emitted at Boyer–Lindquist radius rs outside the ISCO by the sub-Keplerian, mixed radial/azimuthal flow (AART gDisk, P1 Eq. B13). b is the sign of the emitted pʳ, (λ, η) the photon's conserved quantities. sub_kep scales the specific angular momentum (1 is Keplerian), betaphi blends Ω̂ (1) toward the infall Ω̄ (0), betar blends uʳ from 0 (1) toward pure radial infall (0); the defaults give the Keplerian equatorial disk.

References

BlackLightPlus.Matter.plasma_state — Method
plasma_state(src::EquatorialHotspot, st, x, g, params) -> PlasmaState

The spot plasma at x, with g = (gcov, gcon) the metric pair of st at x. The fluid moves on the Keplerian circular orbit through x, with the unit toroidal field direction; the spot weight is G = exp(−ds²/2R_spot²) for ds² < (4 R_spot)², else zero, with ds² the rest-frame proper separation from the centre at t = x⁰. Density ambient_n_e + n_e0 G; temperature and field the density-weighted blends of the ambient and spot values. vacuum outside the cut without ambient plasma or at zero density. Where the circular flow at the Keplerian Ω of the Kerr-Schild radius of x is not timelike (den > 0 in circular_velocity, as inside the photon orbit), the square root of the four-velocity throws a DomainError. sigma and beta_inv are zero; params is not read.

BlackLightPlus.Matter.circular_velocity — Method
circular_velocity(gcov, x, Omega) -> (ucon, ucov, den)

Four-velocity u^μ = u^t (ξ + Ω η) of the circular flow of coordinate angular velocity Omega at x, normalised to u·u = −1, its covariant form, and den = X_tt + 2Ω X_tφ + Ω² X_φφ = −1/(u^t)². BlackLightPlus.Matter.constant_l_potential — Method
constant_l_potential(Xtt, Xtp, Xpp, l0) -> (W, Omega, den)

Effective potential of a flow of constant specific angular momentum ℓ = −u_φ/u_t = l0, in the publication sign of Abramowicz, Jaroszyński & Sikora (1978): W = ln|u_t|, minimal at the pressure maximum and increasing outward. With Ω = −(X_tφ + ℓ X_tt)/(X_φφ + ℓ X_tφ) the flow's angular velocity, W = ln|X_tt + Ω X_tφ| − ½ ln|den| and den = X_tt + 2Ω X_tφ + Ω² X_φφ, negative where the flow is defined.

BlackLightPlus.Matter.toroidal_direction — Method
toroidal_direction(gcov, x, ell, ucon, ucov) -> SVector{4}

Unit spacelike field direction (b·b = +1, b·u = 0) of a toroidal field for a flow of specific angular momentum ell: b ∝ ℓ ξ + η projected orthogonal to u. On the rotation axis η → 0 and the projection degenerates (b·b ≤ 1e-10); ∂_x projected orthogonal to u is used instead, so b stays finite. The pitch angle is undefined there and only the scalar field strength carries weight.
