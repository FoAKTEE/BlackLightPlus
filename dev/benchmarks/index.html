Benchmarks

Measured performance of the package on one host, anta: 2× Intel Xeon Platinum 8360Y (72 cores, 144 hardware threads) and 2× NVIDIA A100 80 GB, CUDA 13, Julia 1.12.6. The protocol is in bench/README.md: a first call (the JIT compilation and, on a GPU, the uploads to a fresh device pool) followed by three calls whose best wall time is the result; imaging throughput is pixels per wall second (the ray count for render_amr), Monte Carlo throughput superphotons per wall second, primaries plus scattered children. The GPU legs ran inside a scheduler allocation of four hardware threads; both GPUs are bitwise equal to one on every leg. The full tables with their conditions are in the Verification record (§4) and in bench/results/ on the blp branch.

One deck, three backends

The deck: the AthenaK torus on KerrSchildCartesian(0.9), 230 GHz, inclination 60°, field of view 30 M, the plasma model of the paper's figures.

The executors size their chunks from the item count, the output size and the device and thread counts, so no leg depends on a chunk setting; on one A100 the automatic chunk matches or beats the best chunk of a sweep on every leg.

Against the reference implementation

On identical decks the package and the reference implementation it replaced (the blp branch) are bitwise equal on the same A100; the one-A100 geometric-mean speed ratio of the package over the reference is 1.13 at the defaults and 1.06 at each code's best setting, and on the CPU the package is 1.4–2.3× faster. The remaining gaps on the device are kernel register pressure, recorded by bench/kernel_audit.jl against its baseline.

Running the benchmarks

R=bench/results
BLP_OUT=$R/raw/cpu_t16.jsonl julia -t 16 --project=. bench/bench.jl       # CPU legs
sbatch bench/slurm/run_gpu.sh                                               # GPU legs
julia --project=. bench/bench.jl merge                                      # dated JSON and table
python3 bench/plot_performance.py                                           # the figure

bench/bench.jl and bench/reference.jl list the legs, the decks, the protocol and every environment variable in their headers (BLP_LEGS, BLP_OUT, BLP_TEST_GRMHD for the snapshot path). The benchmark results are not part of this branch.
