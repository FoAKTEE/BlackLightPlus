with the number or tolerance exactly as it stands in the source. Status: *checked by test*
= asserted by `@test` in the suite; *measured benchmark* = recorded measurement on
2x NVIDIA A100 80 GB, 2x Intel Xeon Platinum 8360Y (72 cores, 144 hardware threads), CUDA 13.2, Julia 1.12.6; warm (JIT excluded), best-of-N, legs run sequentially and exclusively (docs/src/benchmarks.md:3-8); *[OPEN]* = no supporting evidence in the repository; *recorded run* = value in a saved run record of `figures/out/eht_bbh/` (gitignored output of `figures/fig_eht_bbh.jl` at bb85b21, checked in the working tree); *cross-code (docs)* = RAPTOR-fork comparison result stated in `docs/src/validation.md` from JSON under `results/blp/raptor/evidence/`.
