Scope: one convention per quantity for the manuscript. "Chosen" is what the paper writes;
"Code" is what `src/` does (file:line); the last column gives each source's convention and the
map to the chosen one. Source keys: KP = KPolaris (ref-paper/arxiv-2609.26298/tex/ms.tex),

## Manuscript symbols that differ from the table

The manuscript keeps one meaning per symbol: tensor indices are $\mu,\nu,\alpha,\beta$
(unindexed $\alpha,\beta$ are screen coordinates), and a subscript $\nu$ on $I_\nu$, $j_\nu$,
$\alpha_\nu$, $\rho_\nu$, $\mathcal B_\nu$ marks a quantity per unit frequency; coherency tensor
$\mathcal N^{\mu\nu}$ (`\Ncoh`; $N$ and $N^i$ are the lapse and shift); binary hole index $\varpi$
($f_\varpi$, $l^{(\varpi)}_\mu$, $m_\varpi$, $a_\varpi$; a roman subscript h means horizon); power-law index $p_{\rm pl}$ (`\ppl`),
$\kappa$-distribution index $\kappa_{\rm d}$ and width $w_{\rm d}$ (`\kapd`), Planck function
$\mathcal B_\nu$ (`\Bnu`), adiabatic indices $\hat\gamma_{\rm i},\hat\gamma_{\rm e}$, electron
temperature $T_{\rm e}$, $R=[R_{\rm high}(\beta_{\rm p}/\beta_{\rm crit})^2+R_{\rm low}]/[1+(\beta_{\rm p}/\beta_{\rm crit})^2]$,
thermal $\rho_Q$ fitting function $\Psi_{\rm m}(X)$ (M21's $f_m$), scattering bias $\zeta$
(`\bias`, D09's $b$), transported vector $Z^\mu$, polarized part $\boldsymbol{\mathcal S}_{\rm P}$
(`\SvecP`), segment count $N_{\rm seg}$ (`\Nseg`), unpolarized segment emission $J_m$ and
transmission $e^{-\Delta\tau_m}$, redshift factor written $\nu_{\rm obs}/\nu$.

## Open items

- **[OPEN-1] Ray time-orientation — resolved.** The camera launched the covector
  $(k_t,k_i)=(-1,\hat n_{\rm in})$ and integrated it forward: a future-directed ingoing
  null ray, the $(t,\phi)\to(-t,-\phi)$ image of the received photon. It gave
  $L_z/E=+\alpha\sin i$ (image mirrored, $\alpha_{\rm B}=-\alpha_{\rm img}$), $-k_\mu u^\mu=\gamma(1+v)$
  for gas approaching the camera instead of $\gamma(1-v)$, mirrored $U$ and $V$, and
  slow-light emission times $t_{\rm obs}-x^t$ on a ray whose $x^t$ ran forward. The camera now
  stores $p=-k$ with $k$ the received covector ($k_t=-1$, outward), every consumer reads
  $k=-p$, and the camera-to-Bardeen map is the identity. Tests: 3d76f99
  (test/test_ray_orientation.jl, 17/64 before, 64/64 after); fix: 706b55f. The backward ray
  approaches the future horizon asymptotically in ingoing Kerr–Schild coordinates, where the
  fused polarized kernel's transported frame degrades. Every CPU and device march ends on
  the shared rule of 47527a4 (horizon backstop $10^{-3}$, redshift cut $E_n>10^2$), up to which
  $-k\cdot u_{\rm cam}$ stays within 1%; the user-facing statement of these conventions is the
  Conventions section of docs/src/method.md.
- **[OPEN-2] Fluid-frame Q basis versus coefficient basis — resolved, no code change.**
  The tetrad's $Q$ axis ($e_{(1)}$, perpendicular to the $k$–$B$ plane) and the M21
  coefficient basis ($Q>0$ in the $k$–$B$ plane, $j_Q<0$) differ by $90^\circ$ and no sign
  flip is applied (coefficients.jl:341; Blacklight simulation_coefficients.cpp:493 alike).
  The camera readout applies the same tetrad rule, so the code evolves the physical
  coherency tensor rotated by $90^\circ$ about $k$ and reads it out in $(e_{(1)},e_{(2)})$; this
  equals the physical Stokes vector in $(e_{(2)},-e_{(1)})$, which is the M21 basis in the fluid
  frame and (north, east) at the camera. A rotation about $k$ commutes with parallel
  transport and leaves $\rho_V$, $V$ and the transfer-matrix form unchanged, so no
  $\chi\to\chi+90^\circ$ error occurs. The thin-slab tests in test/test_ray_orientation.jl fix
  the absolute EVPA, $U$ handedness, $V$ sign and Faraday sense against IEEE/IAU.
- The Mahakala camera source (arXiv:2304.03804) and GPUmonty (arXiv:2602.13198) are cited
  in src/ comments but missing from docs/references.bib.
