"Code" is what `src/` does (file:line); the last column gives each source's convention and the
map to the chosen one. Source keys: KP = KPolaris (ref-paper/arxiv-2609.26298/tex/ms.tex),

## Open items

- **[OPEN-1] Ray time-orientation.** Covector at the camera $(k_t,k_i)=(-1,\hat n_{\rm in})$
  integrated forward (camera.jl:48–55) is a future-directed ingoing null ray. The physical
  backward ray (W22, S23, MG18) carries $(-1,-\hat n_{\rm in})$ integrated to $\lambda<0$,
  equivalently $(+1,\hat n_{\rm in})$ forward. In flat space the code gives
  $-k_\mu u^\mu=\gamma(1+v)$ for fluid moving toward the camera, where the physical value is
  $\gamma(1-v)$. In Kerr the traced ray is the $(t,\phi)\to(-t,-\phi)$ image of the physical
  ray at $-\alpha_{\rm img}$, which test/test_analytic_kerr.jl:17–22 records as
  $\alpha_{\rm B}=-\alpha_{\rm img}$. For purely azimuthal flow this is an exact left–right
  mirror of the image; the Doppler sign of $u^r,u^\theta$ is also reversed, which a mirror does
  not undo. Slow light uses $t_{\rm emit}=t_{\rm obs}-X^0$ (src/simulation/time_series.jl:7–8).
  This needs an orchestrator decision: change the code (flip the launch covector), or state
  the convention and its limits in the paper. Fallback until then: the paper states rays in
  the code's form and reports images in $(\alpha_{\rm img},\beta_{\rm img})$.
- **[OPEN-2] Fluid-frame Q basis versus coefficient basis.** The fluid tetrad follows
  MG18/W22 ($e_{(2)}$ in the $k$–$B$ plane, $Q>0$ along $e_{(1)}$, perpendicular to it;
  tetrads.jl:3–6, integrator.jl:37), while the coefficients follow M21 ($Q>0$ in the $k$–$B$
  plane, $j_Q<0$; coefficients.jl:341). M21 §2 states that changing between these bases flips
  every $Q$ and $U$ coefficient. No flip is applied (the same holds in Blacklight,
  simulation_coefficients.cpp:493). Taken by itself this is a global $\chi\to\chi+90^\circ$
  ($Q,U\to-Q,-U$; $I,V$ unchanged) at the camera. The net camera-to-IAU map (OPEN-1 ×
  OPEN-2 × the readout-axis relabelling in the Stokes row) has not been checked against an
  independent polarized code. No such comparison exists in test/ or docs/src/benchmarks.md,
  so the paper must not assert an absolute EVPA or $V$ sign until one is made. Fallback:
  report $|V|$, $m_{\rm net}$, and EVPA differences, which do not depend on the sign.
- The Mahakala camera source (arXiv:2304.03804) and GPUmonty (arXiv:2602.13198) are cited
  in src/ comments but missing from docs/references.bib.
