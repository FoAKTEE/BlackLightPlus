- Declarations (literature-grounded; line numbers in tex/main.tex):
  - RAPTOR as described here: adaptive Runge–Kutta–Fehlberg geodesic integrator; Christoffel symbols analytic or on-the-fly 4th-order centred finite differences; hybrid ImEx polarized integrator; adaptive camera; BHAC AMR interface; thermal, κ and power-law coefficients (l.1235).
  - Test 1 (analytic, flat, constant coefficients; Dexter 2016 / Mościbrodzka 2018): emission/absorption $j_I=2,j_Q=1,\alpha_I=1,\alpha_Q=1.2$; rotation $j_{Q,U,V}=0.1,\rho_Q=10,\rho_V=-4$ (l.1244–1273); transfer matrix l.1247–1258. RAPTOR run with RK4 and IE variants (l.1402). Criterion: codes match analytic solution to better than $10^{-5}$ (l.1579).
  - Test 2 (thin disk, Novikov–Thorne/Page–Thorne flux, colour correction $n=1.8$, Chandrasekhar Table 24 polarization in disk plane, emission $r_{\rm ISCO}$–$100r_g$, Keplerian $u^\phi/u^t=1/(r^{3/2}+a)$): $a=0.99$, $M=10M_\odot$, $D=0.05$ pc, $\dot M=0.1\dot M_{\rm Edd}$, $h\nu=1$ keV; camera $\theta_{\rm cam}=75^\circ$, NX = 80, DX = 40 $r_g$, FOV = 78.99 μas (l.1278–1331). Criterion: NMSE ≤ 1.5% between codes (l.1579). RAPTOR: $F=6.8689\times10^6$ Jy, $m_{\rm net}=2.3265\%$, $\langle|m|\rangle=2.3726\%$, EVPA $=88.025^\circ$ (l.1464).
  - Camera convention (shared with Gold+2020): tetrad such that the central ray has $k_\phi=0$; $\theta_{\rm cam}$ from BH spin axis; $R_{\rm cam}=10^4r_g$; pixels = evenly spaced geodesics at pixel centres (80 px over 80 μas → −39.5…+39.5 μas); square images, equal W–E and N–S FOV (l.1313–1321).
  - Test 3 (iharm SANE snapshot, $a=0.9375$, 288×128×128, $t=4500\,r_g/c$, fast light): $M=6.2\times10^9M_\odot$, $D=16.9$ Mpc, 230 GHz, $\theta_{\rm cam}=163^\circ$, $\phi_{\rm cam}=0$, FOV 160 μas, DX = 44.17 $r_g$, NX = 160; $\rho_{\rm CGS}=\mathcal M\rho_{\rm code}/r_g^3$, $\mathcal M=1.672\times10^{26}$ g (≈0.50 Jy unpolarized, ≈0.47 Jy polarized) (l.1336–1371); $T_e=2m_pu/(15k\rho)$ ≡ $R_{\rm low}=R_{\rm high}=3$ (l.1376–1380); emission only where $\sigma=B^2/\rho<1$ (l.1384); Dexter 2016 coefficient fits for all codes (l.1614). RAPTOR: $F=0.49396$ Jy, $m_{\rm net}=1.6057\%$, $v_{\rm net}=0.67381\%$, $\langle|m|\rangle=31.893\%$, EVPA $=-77.797^\circ$, $|\beta_2|=0.28346$, $\angle\beta_2=-21.31^\circ$ (l.1515).
  - Metrics: $F=\sum I_j$, $m_{\rm net}=\sqrt{(\sum Q)^2+(\sum U)^2}/\sum I$, $\langle|m|\rangle=\sum\sqrt{Q_j^2+U_j^2}/\sum I$, $v_{\rm net}=\sum V/\sum I$ (l.1418–1423); $\beta_2$ (Palumbo+2020) about the image centre (l.1425–1430); 20 μas FWHM Gaussian blur before $\langle|m|\rangle$ and $\beta_2$ (l.1432); EVPA East of North (l.1434–1437); ${\rm NMSE}(A,B)=\sum|A_j-B_j|^2/\sum|A_j|^2$, asymmetric, same normalization as Gold+2020 (l.1442–1447); NMLE, SSIM/DSSIM, ZNCC/DZNCC definitions (App. l.1637–1645).
  - Agreement achieved (acceptance template): GRMHD NMSE worst case 0.02 ($I$), 0.045 (linear pol.), 0.13 ($V$) (l.1555, l.1581); max inter-code spread vs EHT $1\sigma$: $m_{\rm net}$ 0.21% vs 1.35%, $v_{\rm net}$ 0.08% vs 0.4%, $\langle|m|\rangle$ 0.70% vs 2.5%, $|\beta_2|$ 0.0026 vs 0.015, $\angle\beta_2$ 3.4° vs 17° (all < 0.3σ) (l.1529–1548); translated to parameter resolution: $M$ 0.4%, $\mathcal M$ 9%, inclination 0.8°, $R_{\rm high}$ 0.18 (l.1557).
  - Fit-function sensitivity: Pandya+2016 vs Dexter 2016 changes $v_{\rm net}$ by 0.13 pts, $\langle|m|\rangle$ by 1.8 pts, $|\beta_2|$ by 0.016 (larger than code spread), driven by $j_V$; Dexter $\rho_V$ unstable for $\Theta_e<1$; approximating $K_n$ corrupts $\rho_V$ at low $T$ (l.1614–1630).
