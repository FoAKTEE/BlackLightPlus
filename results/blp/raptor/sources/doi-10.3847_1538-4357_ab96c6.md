  - Transfer equation in invariant form $d(I_\nu/\nu^3)/d\lambda=j_\nu/\nu^2-\nu\alpha_\nu(I_\nu/\nu^3)$, $ds\to\nu\,d\lambda$ (l.197–202, eq. 2); geodesics $dx^\mu/d\lambda=k^\mu$, $dk^\mu/d\lambda=-\Gamma^\mu_{\alpha\beta}k^\alpha k^\beta$ (l.204–213, eqs. 3–4); pixel flux $F_\nu\approx I_\nu\Delta\Omega$ (l.216). No scattering (Thomson depth ≲ 1e-4) (l.181).
  - RAPTOR as described: Verlet (2nd order) or RK2/RK4 geodesic integrators; transfer "backward" along rays as they are constructed; C with OpenACC for CPU/GPU; thermal and κ synchrotron; BHAC (uniform and AMR), HARM2D, HARM3D input; full-sky VR images (l.270–272) [p.5].
  - Test A — equatorial Kerr deflection angle vs impact parameter against Iyer & Hansen 2009 (l.280–284, l.584–587, Fig. 1 l.346–352) [p.7]: residuals ~1e-4 attributed to finite camera distance.
  - Test B — five analytic imaging tests (l.286–409) [pp.6–9]: Kerr, BL coordinates, $G=c=\hbar=1$; $i=60^\circ$; $M=6\times10^{11}$ cm (~4e6 $M_\odot$); $d=2.4\times10^{22}$ cm (7.78 kpc) (l.288–290). $n=n_0\exp\{-\tfrac12[(r/10)^2+z^2]\}$, $z=h\cos\theta$ (eq. 5); $l=(l_0/(1+R))R^{1+q}$, $R=r\sin\theta$, $q=0.5$ (eq. 6); $u_\mu=\bar u(-1,0,0,l)$, $\bar u=\sqrt{-(g^{tt}-2g^{t\phi}l+g^{\phi\phi}l^2)^{-1}}$ (eqs. 7–8); $j_\nu=Cn(\nu/\nu_p)^{-\alpha}$, $Cn_0=3\times10^{-18}$ cgs, $\nu_p=230$ GHz (eq. 9); $\alpha_\nu=ACn(\nu/\nu_p)^{-(\beta+\alpha)}$, $\beta=2.5$ (eq. 11). Parameters (Table 1, l.354–368): T1 $(A,\alpha,h,l_0,a)=(0,-3,0,0,0.9)$; T2 $(0,-2,0,1,0)$; T3 $(0,0,10/3,1,0.9)$; T4 $(10^5,0,10/3,1,0.9)$; T5 $(10^6,0,100/3,1,0.9)$.
