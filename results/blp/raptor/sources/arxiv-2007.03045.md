# arxiv-2007.03045 — Bronzwaer, Younsi, Davelaar & Falcke 2020, RAPTOR II: Polarized radiative transfer in curved spacetime (A&A 641, A126)

- Role: baseline (polarized RAPTOR: polarization-vector transport, tetrad, Stokes conventions, ImEx plasma integrator, thermal Dexter-2016 coefficients).
- Declarations (literature-grounded; line numbers in tex/RAPTOR_II_AA.tex):
  - Stokes convention: IAU/IEEE (Hamaker 1996); EVPA $\chi=\tfrac12\arctan(U/Q)$ measured East of North; circular polarization right-handed when the EVPA rotates clockwise for an observer looking along the propagation direction (l.159).
  - Invariant Stokes $\boldsymbol{\mathcal S}=\mathbf S_\nu/\nu^3$ (l.174–178); invariant coefficients $j/\nu^2$, $\alpha\nu$, $\rho\nu$ (l.848–855).
  - Ray state: $(\mathcal I,\mathcal I_{\rm pol},f^\mu)$ with complex unit polarization vector $f^\mu f^*_\mu=1$, parallel-transported $k^\alpha\nabla_\alpha f^\mu=0$ (l.182–190, l.203–205). Map to Stokes: $\mathcal Q=\mathcal I_{\rm pol}(f^{(1)}f^{(1)*}-f^{(2)}f^{(2)*})$, $\mathcal U=\mathcal I_{\rm pol}(f^{(1)}f^{(2)*}+f^{(2)}f^{(1)*})$, $\mathcal V=i\,\mathcal I_{\rm pol}(f^{(1)}f^{(2)*}-f^{(2)}f^{(1)*})$ (l.206–223); inverse with $f^{(1)}\in\mathbb R$: $f^{(1)}=\sqrt{(1+\tilde Q)/2}$, $f^{(2)}=(\tilde U-i\tilde V)/(2f^{(1)})$ (l.226–238).
  - Vacuum propagation: $df^\mu/d\lambda=-\Gamma^\mu_{\alpha\rho}k^\alpha f^\rho$, $d\mathcal I/d\lambda=d\mathcal I_{\rm pol}/d\lambda=0$; $\lambda$ in units of $GM/c^2$ (l.279–287).
  - Tetrad (generalized Gammie & Leung 2012): indices $(t,\parallel,\perp,K)\leftrightarrow(t,x,y,z)$, right-handed (l.297); $\omega=-k_\alpha u^\alpha$, $\beta=u_\alpha d^\alpha$, $\mathcal C=k_\alpha d^\alpha/\omega-\beta$ [printed as $/g$], $\mathcal N=\sqrt{d^2+\beta^2-\mathcal C^2}$ (l.300–308); $e_{(t)}=u$, $e_{(K)}=k/\omega-u$, $e_{(\parallel)}=(d+\beta u-\mathcal C e_{(K)})/\mathcal N$, $e_{(\perp)}=\epsilon^{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}u_\nu k_\alpha d_\beta/(\omega\mathcal N)$ with $\epsilon^{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}=-[\mu\nu\alpha\beta]/\sqrt{-g}$ (l.310–321). Plasma frame: $u$ = fluid four-velocity, $d=b^\mu$ (l.297); observer frame: static $u_\mu=(u_t,0,0,0)$, trial $d$ chosen to satisfy IAU/IEEE (l.297).
  - With $e_{(\parallel)}\parallel b$: $j_U=\alpha_U=\rho_U=0$ (l.376).
  - Plasma transfer matrix (standard Landi Degl'Innocenti form, rows $(\alpha_I,\alpha_Q,\alpha_U,\alpha_V)$, $(\alpha_Q,\alpha_I,\rho_V,-\rho_U)$, $(\alpha_U,-\rho_V,\alpha_I,\rho_Q)$, $(\alpha_V,\rho_U,-\rho_Q,\alpha_I)$) (l.332–358).
  - Operator split per step: plasma interaction first, then vacuum transport step; plasma step skipped in vacuum (l.369).
  - Geodesic integration backward from camera with RK4 until a stopping condition (horizon plunge or GRMHD outer boundary); polarized transfer then proceeds forward toward the camera; RK4 extended to $f^\alpha$ (l.408–423).
  - Plasma integrator: explicit RK4 (l.380–393) switched per step to implicit trapezoid (2nd order) solved by explicit LU decomposition (l.394–399, App. l.694–773); stiffness test: RK4 stable iff $|1+\zeta+\zeta^2/2+\zeta^3/6+\zeta^4/24|<1$, $\zeta=\Delta\lambda\Lambda$ for all four eigenvalues $\Lambda=\alpha_I\pm\sqrt{z_\pm}$ (App. l.645–690).
  - Thermal coefficients adapted from Dexter 2016 with Mościbrodzka & Gammie (ipole) fit modifications (l.331, l.781): $j_I,j_Q\propto\frac{n_ee^2\nu}{2\sqrt3c\theta_e^2}I_{I,Q}(x)$, $j_V=\frac{2n_ee^2\nu}{3\sqrt3c\theta_e^3\tan\theta_B}I_V(x)$, $\nu_c=\frac{3eB\sin\theta_B\theta_e^2}{4\pi m_ec}$, fit functions $I_I,I_Q,I_V$ (l.783–809); Kirchhoff absorption (l.810–814); $\rho_Q,\rho_V$ with $f_m(\tilde X)$, $\Delta J_5(\tilde X)$, $\tilde X=(\frac{3}{2\sqrt2}10^{-3}\nu/\nu_c)^{-1/2}$ (l.816–846). $j_Q$ is printed with a positive sign.
  - Verification: flat-space analytic plasma tests (test 1: $j_I=2,j_Q=1,\alpha_I=1,\alpha_Q=1.2$; test 2: $j_{Q,U,V}=0.1,\rho_Q=10,\rho_V=-4$), RK4 error $\propto\Delta\lambda^4$, trapezoid $\propto\Delta\lambda^2$ (l.433–441, Figs. l.488–497); thin-disk (Schnittman & Krolik 2009) polarized test with $\theta_{\rm cam}=75^\circ$, $a=0.99$, $M=10M_\odot$, $d=0.05$ pc, $\nu=2.418\times10^{17}$ Hz, 80² px, DX = 40 $R_G$, $r_{\rm cam}=10^4R_G$ (l.516–567); HARM GRMHD test, $a=0.94$ [sic], $M=6.2\times10^9M_\odot$, 16.9 Mpc, 230 GHz, $\theta_{\rm cam}=163^\circ$, 160² px, DX = 44.17 $R_G$, $\mathcal M_{\rm low}=1.672\times10^{26}$ g, $\mathcal M_{\rm high}=2.739\times10^{25}$ g, $T_p/T_e=3$ (l.543–567, l.592). Fluxes: thin disk $I=6.869\times10^6$ Jy; GRMHD high $I=0.494$, $Q=-0.00722$, $U=-0.00328$, $V=0.00333$ Jy (l.523–538). RK4 transport convergence tracked via $|f|$ conservation (l.575).
  - Agreement with ipole on these tests: order 1% mean squared error (l.596).
