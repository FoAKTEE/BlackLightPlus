# arxiv-2403.13308v3 — Combi & Ressler 2024, a binary black hole metric approximation from inspiral to merger
- Mirror: ref-paper/arxiv-2403.13308v3/ (PROVENANCE.md; main tex ms.tex). Metric code: gitlab.com/combi.luciano/analyticalbbh (bbl key softcombi), implemented in Athena++/AthenaK and the Einstein Toolkit (l.353).
- Role: the superposed Kerr-Schild (SKS) metric that `smressle-blacklight_binaries` implements (`boosted_BH_metric_addition`, `get_prime_coords`) and that the AthenaK `dynbbh` dumps imaged by BLP were evolved on; the standard against which BLP's static `SuperposedKerrSchild` is judged.
- Declarations (literature-grounded):
  - Kerr-Schild hole with arbitrary spin vector $a^i$: $g_{ab}=\eta_{ab}+2Hl_al_b$ (l.159–161, eq:ksmetric), $l_adX^a=dt+\frac{1}{r^2+a^2}[rX^i-\epsilon^i{}_{jk}a^jX^k+\frac{(a\cdot X)}{r}a^i]dX_i$ (l.163–166, eq:covectorBH), $r^2=\tfrac12(R^2-a^2)+[\tfrac14(R^2-a^2)^2+(a\cdot X)^2]^{1/2}$ (l.167–170), $H=Mr^3/(r^4+(a\cdot X)^2)$ (l.172–175, eq:hfun). The fork's `fprime = q 2r'^3/(r'^4+(a\cdot x')^2)` and `l_upperprime` are these formulas verbatim.
  - Boost: $\Lambda(\beta^i)$ with $\Lambda^0{}_i=-\gamma\beta n_i$, $\Lambda^i{}_j=\delta_{ij}+(\gamma-1)n_in_j$ (l.203–224, eq:lorentz); inverse transformation to the hole frame with $\bar x^i=x^i-s^i(t)$ and the acceleration term dropped from the Jacobian, so $\partial X^a/\partial x^b=\Lambda^a{}_b+O(\dot\beta)$ (l.241–257, eq:fermicoord2); the time coordinate $T$ is not needed because each hole term is stationary in its own frame (appendix A, l.644–670).
  - SKS metric $g_{ab}=\eta_{ab}+[2H\Lambda^d{}_al_d\Lambda^c{}_bl_c]_{(1)}+[\dots]_{(2)}$ (l.259–263, eq:sks). Dropping the boost is a first-order error in $\beta$ even at large separation (l.265).
  - Free parameters per hole: spin vector, mass, trajectory, 3-velocity (l.269). Trajectories from 4PN equations of motion (CBwaves), linear interpolation of an HDF5 table in time (l.271–300, l.351).
  - Merger: masses, spins and velocities of the two terms interpolated to the remnant by $\mathcal W(t)$ over $t_{\rm merger}\pm dt_{\rm buffer}$ (l.302–337, appendix B).
  - Numerical use: the metric is time dependent; GRMHD codes re-evaluate it every step or every ~10 steps for $v\sim0.1c$ (l.582).
- Consequences for a ray tracer: the metric at a point is $g(t,x)$; a ray's samples span $\Delta t\sim$ the light-crossing time of the near zone, during which each hole moves $v\,\Delta t\sim$ a few $M$; `results/blp/binaries/checks/frozen_vs_moving.jl` measures what freezing $g$ at one time does to the horizon and the shadow.
